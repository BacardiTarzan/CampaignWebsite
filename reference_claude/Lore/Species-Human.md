# Human

Humans are by far the most common species in Aldermark, and they have shaped the region's politics, languages, and conflicts more than any other. The starting region of Aldermark is organized into several feudal holds — small villages and farming hamlets under the protection of local lords, with proper cities further afield. Travel between holds means roads patrolled by some guards and prowled by some bandits, and it is common to hear that the lord of one valley is feuding with the lord of the next.

The longer-lived species tend to view human politics with bemused exasperation. To an elf or a dwarf, watching humans war over a stretch of grazing land they will all be forgotten by within three generations is hard to take seriously. Humans, in turn, find the long-lived species' patience faintly insulting.

Most humans in Aldermark are farmers, laborers, smiths, brewers, tavern keepers, and the like — simple people living lives shaped by weather, harvest, and lord. Their sense of the wider world is small. They believe in their gods because their priest does. They believe in dragons the way one believes in any old story, and the few who truly believe are the same villagers who swear there are fairies in the well. Many have never met a High Elf and aren't sure those exist either.

This makes humans, despite being the most numerous species, often the most surprised when the world turns out to be larger than they thought.

## Adventuring Hooks

* A village to defend, a lord to serve, or a lord to escape
* A debt owed to a person who has just shown up to collect it
* A relic dug up under your church that should not be there
* The simple draw of a road that might lead somewhere
