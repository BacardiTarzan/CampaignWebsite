# Halfling

Halflings are everywhere in Aldermark, and almost nowhere visible. They live among other species in nearly every village, town, and keep — but they also keep their own communities, tucked into rolling hill country, riverbanks, and quiet valleys. A traveler may walk through a halfling village of a few hundred and notice only a fence here, a low door there, a cottage that looked at first glance like a haystack.

Halflings are charming, easy to like, and very hard to dislike for long. They tell good stories, drink generously, and have a near-magical talent for showing up exactly when there's good food to be had. They are also a little tricksy — not malicious, but they take a quiet delight in mild mischief, and a halfling at a market is rarely the worse for the visit, even if the seller can't quite say why.

Halflings get along with almost everyone. They are welcome among dwarves, Wood Elves, humans, and even orc and goliath communities. Somewhere in a High Elf enclave, there is almost certainly a wine-guzzling halfling whose stories the elves enjoy more than they will admit.

A halfling who leaves home rarely does so out of need. Most go because they want to *see*.

## Adventuring Hooks
- Wanderlust, plain and simple
- A tall tale you swore was true that turned out to be even more true than you knew
- An old debt of hospitality
- A relative who got into something bigger than themselves
