# Tiefling

Tieflings are far rarer than humans assume them to be, but more numerous than aasimar. Most are not the result of recent infernal pacts — most are echoes of an age long past, when beings of the Lower Planes saw Aldermark as a place to entertain their vile proclivities. That blood lingers, sometimes for generations, before surfacing in a child whose horns and tail terrify the village they were born into.

The reception is almost always fear, and often worse. In small villages, parents have been known to abandon tiefling children at the edge of the woods. Priests have been known to attempt "exorcisms" that the child does not survive. Even those who are raised by their families often grow up half-hidden, kept indoors when strangers visit.

A tiefling who reaches adulthood in Aldermark has, almost without exception, learned to keep moving, keep quiet, or keep dangerous company. Some find shelter in cities, where strangeness is more easily absorbed. Some find each other. Most do not.

The deeper truth — that most tieflings carry blood from a contact that happened centuries before their birth — is unknown to common folk and only rumored among scholars. The slow, recent uptick in tiefling births has not gone unnoticed by those few who track such things, and it tracks suspiciously close to the slow, recent uptick in aasimar births.

Something, it seems, is reaching back into the world.

## Adventuring Hooks

* Survival — a road is safer than the village that wants to "save" you
* Discovering what bloodline you actually carry
* A pact, ancient or new, that someone is trying to call due
* A purpose that has nothing to do with what you were born as
