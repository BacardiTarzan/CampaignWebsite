# Gnome

Gnomes are inventors, tinkerers, illusionists, and curious folk by nature. They rarely build their own settlements in Aldermark; instead they fold themselves into the communities of others — a gnome clockmaker in a human town, a gnome herbalist living among Wood Elves, a gnome lecturer welcomed at a dwarven academy of artifice.

Most communities are glad to have them. Gnomes bring mechanical novelty, magical tricks, and a sharp eye for problems no one else has noticed. They are easy to underestimate, which they tend to find amusing.

There are gnome communities somewhere — small, tucked into hillsides or hollow groves — but most of the people of Aldermark have never seen one and never will. Gnomes do not advertise.

A gnome who leaves home usually does so because the question they're chasing is bigger than the workshop they grew up in.
Gnomes adapt to their environment in a curious way. Those who live in the wilderness pick up a touch of fey magic from their surroundings; those raised in cities and stone-wrought caverns develop a keenness for machinery and the mending of crafted goods.

## Adventuring Hooks

* An invention that requires field testing under genuinely dangerous conditions
* A mystery whose pieces you've been collecting for years
* A favor owed to a community that took you in
* A rumor about a gnomish settlement you've never been able to find
