# Dragonborn

Dragonborn are rare in Aldermark and rarer still outside their tribal communities. They have no single homeland — wherever a dragon's bloodline took root in a distant age, a tribe eventually formed nearby. Some live in remote highlands, others in volcanic foothills, river deltas, or ash plains matching their ancestor's nature.

The first dragonborn were not born — they were *made*. In an age before written history, the early High Elves studied dragon magic alongside their patrons, and that arcane bond produced the first of their kind. Whether they were intended as servants, soldiers, or simply emerged as a consequence of magical experiments is a question even dragons themselves answer differently.

Today's dragonborn live in small, scattered tribes, with strong ties to the dragon their bloodline traces back to. They know the truth about dragons — that dragons are real, ancient, and present — which most humans of Aldermark do not. Among the people of this region, only dragonborn, the oldest elves, and dwarven loremasters speak of dragons as anything but children's tales.

A dragonborn walking through a market town draws stares but rarely violence. Few common folk know what they are; most assume some kind of foreign reptilian people from far away. They are not feared the way a tiefling or drow might be — they are simply a curiosity, until they prove otherwise.

## Adventuring Hooks

* Tribal exile or honor-debt
* A pilgrimage to find your bloodline's dragon — if it still lives
* Recovering something stolen from your tribe long ago
* Curiosity about a world that has forgotten you exist
