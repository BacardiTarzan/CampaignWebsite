# Orc

Orcs in Aldermark are nomadic tribespeople, moving across plains, hill country, and the edges of the great forests. Their tribes are small but tightly bonded — orcs feel a loyalty to one another that few other species can understand by witnessing alone. An orc in your tribe is closer than blood; an orc taken into another community is, given time, the same.

Most settled communities are slow to accept them. Orcs are physically imposing, their features unfamiliar to people whose only exposure has been ugly tales, and a tribe arriving at a town's edge is rarely welcomed without negotiation. But communities that *do* accept orcs almost never regret it. An orc who has been taken in considers that community theirs, and an orc fighting to defend their own is not easily moved.

Orc tribal life centers on shared survival — hunting, herding, raiding when needed, mourning when needed. Their gods are spoken of less openly to outsiders than dwarven gods are; many orc traditions are carried in oral memory rather than written down, and a tribe's particular ways tend to die when the tribe does.

## Adventuring Hooks

* Tribal exile, shame, or a debt you cannot pay at home
* A scout's role that has carried you further than you expected
* A community that took you in — and now needs you
* Hunting something that hunted your tribe first
