# Goliath

Goliaths in Aldermark are rare, and most have hard stories. For ages, goliaths were the slaves and servants of giants — bred for labor, raiding, and war. Some goliaths in the world today are still captive, bound to giant-holds in distant ranges. Those who walk Aldermark are escapees, or descendants of escapees, who have built small free communities of their own.

These communities tend to be tight, defensive, and self-sufficient. Goliaths take care of their own because no one else has, historically, taken care of them. They are also enormously capable — taller than humans, stronger than most species, and built for hard work in harsh country. Most communities they settle near are grateful to have them.

The exception is the dwarves. Dwarves spent ages fighting goliaths over mountain territory, and even though dwarves now know the goliaths fought under their giant masters' lash, the grudge has not faded. Most dwarves treat free goliaths with cool suspicion. Individual dwarves can be friendlier; entire clans, almost never.

Traces of goliath culture from before the binding — gods of giants taken and reshaped, songs of mountain peaks, names of long-dead chieftains — survive in their free villages, but a great deal has been lost.

## Adventuring Hooks
- A relative still captive in a giant-hold
- Defending your community from raiders, slavers, or old masters
- A pilgrimage to a peak or place sacred to your people before the binding
- The simple need to make a place for yourself in a world that didn't choose you
