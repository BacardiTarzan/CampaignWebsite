# The Aldwud

The Aldwud is the great forest east of the holds, stretching for many days' ride across hills, valleys, and one shallow river system. It is old. It was old before the current age began, and what's still standing of it now is the part that survived.

Most travelers see only the Aldwud's edge. The road from Aldwud Keep skirts the wood; the woodcutters of Holmlette work the outer trees and do not go far in. Past the first mile or two, the trees thicken, the paths fade, and the silence becomes the sort that pays attention. Few people who go deep into the Aldwud come back with stories that match each other.

## What's Known

What ordinary folk in the surrounding holds will tell you:

* Wolves and bears live there, as expected. Other things live there too, and what those things are depends on who you ask.
* A band of wood elves keeps a presence somewhere in the wood. They trade in furs, herbs, and tracking work with the surrounding holds. They are uncommon but not unknown — most villages have seen them once or twice.
* An old hag is said to live somewhere in the deep wood. She has lived there for a long time. The stories about her vary; the agreement is that she is not someone you want to find by accident.
* There are ruins in there. Old foundations swallowed by moss. A toppled stone here, a half-buried arch there. Some are said to be older than the age — older even than the dwarven holds. The villagers do not visit them.

## Aldwud Keep

The Keep itself sits at the western edge of the wood, half a day's ride north of Holmlette. It is a modest stone keep with an attached village of a few hundred — a market hall, a small garrison, two churches, a craftsmen's quarter.

**Lord Tolwen** holds Aldwud Keep, swearing allegiance upward to a regional baron whose name Holmlette villagers occasionally remember. Tolwen is in his early forties, a careful administrator more than a warrior, and rules his hold reasonably well by the standards of the region. He is concerned by the news from Holmlette but has many other matters to attend to. A formal request from Father Beldwin or Reeve Aldric would likely earn a small detachment of investigators — or, if such people are on hand, a band of capable strangers paid out of the Keep's coffers.

## Deeper In

What lies deeper in the Aldwud is not common knowledge in the surrounding holds. Adventurers who venture in will find:

* Game trails that fade into nothing
* Patches of forest that feel notably wrong, even to people without training
* Old stones with markings even Father Beldwin cannot read
* Eventually, things and places worth a campaign of their own

The wood does not surrender its secrets easily. It has had a very long time to keep them.
