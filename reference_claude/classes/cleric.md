### Key Features

**Spellcasting (Level 1):** Wisdom-based. Use a Holy Symbol as a focus. Change prepared spells on each Long Rest.

**Divine Order (Level 1):** Choose Protector (Martial weapons + Heavy armor proficiency) or Thaumaturge (extra cantrip + Wisdom modifier bonus to Arcana/Religion checks).

**Channel Divinity (Level 2):** Use twice; regain one on Short Rest, all on Long Rest. Starts with Divine Spark (heal or deal Necrotic/Radiant damage, 1d8 + Wisdom modifier, scaling to 4d8) and Turn Undead (Frighten + Incapacitate Undead within 30 ft.).

**Sear Undead (Level 5):** When using Turn Undead, also deal Radiant damage = [Wisdom modifier]d8 to those who fail.

**Blessed Strikes (Level 7):** Choose Divine Strike (extra 1d8 Necrotic or Radiant on weapon hit) or Potent Spellcasting (add Wisdom modifier to cantrip damage).

**Divine Intervention (Level 9):** Magic Action — cast any Cleric spell of level 5 or lower without a slot or material components; once per Long Rest.

**Improved Blessed Strikes (Level 14):** Divine Strike increases to 2d8; Potent Spellcasting also heals a creature for 2× Wisdom modifier Temp HP when you deal cantrip damage.

**Epic Boon (Level 19):** Gain an Epic Boon feat (Boon of Fate recommended).

**Greater Divine Intervention (Level 20):** Divine Intervention can now select Wish; recharges after 2d4 Long Rests.

### Subclasses

#### Life Domain
*Domain Spells:* Aid, Bless, Cure Wounds, Lesser Restoration (level 3); Mass Healing Word, Revivify (5); Aura of Life, Death Ward (7); Greater Restoration, Mass Cure Wounds (9).
- **Disciple of Life (Level 3):** Healing spells restore additional HP = 2 + spell slot level.
- **Preserve Life (Level 3):** Channel Divinity — restore HP = 5× Cleric level to Bloodied creatures within 30 ft. (up to half max HP each).
- **Blessed Healer (Level 6):** Regain HP = 2 + slot level whenever you cast a healing spell targeting another creature.
- **Supreme Healing (Level 17):** Always use maximum dice results when healing.

#### Light Domain
*Domain Spells:* Burning Hands, Faerie Fire, Scorching Ray, See Invisibility (level 3); Daylight, Fireball (5); Arcane Eye, Wall of Fire (7); Flame Strike, Scrying (9).
- **Radiance of the Dawn (Level 3):** Channel Divinity — flash of light in 30-ft. Emanation dispels magical Darkness; creatures make Con save or take 2d10 + Cleric level Radiant damage.
- **Warding Flare (Level 3):** Reaction — impose Disadvantage on an attack roll. Uses = Wisdom modifier (min 1); recharge on Long Rest.
- **Improved Warding Flare (Level 6):** Recharge on Short or Long Rest; add 2d6 + Wisdom modifier Temp HP to the protected creature.
- **Corona of Light (Level 17):** Magic Action — emit sunlight in 60-ft. radius for 1 minute; enemies in bright light have Disadvantage on saves vs. your Radiance of the Dawn and fire/radiant spells.

#### Trickery Domain
*Domain Spells:* Charm Person, Disguise Self, Invisibility, Pass without Trace (level 3); Hypnotic Pattern, Nondetection (5); Confusion, Dimension Door (7); Dominate Person, Modify Memory (9).
- **Blessing of the Trickster (Level 3):** Grant Advantage on Stealth checks to yourself or a willing creature for 1 Long Rest.
- **Invoke Duplicity (Level 3):** Channel Divinity — create an illusory duplicate within 30 ft.; cast spells from its space, gain Advantage vs. adjacent creatures, move it as a Bonus Action.
- **Trickster's Transposition (Level 6):** Swap places with your duplicate when you move it.
- **Improved Duplicity (Level 17):** Allies also gain Advantage on attacks against creatures near the illusion; illusion heals a creature for Cleric level HP when it ends.

#### War Domain
*Domain Spells:* Guiding Bolt, Magic Weapon, Shield of Faith, Spiritual Weapon (level 3); Crusader's Mantle, Spirit Guardians (5); Fire Shield, Freedom of Movement (7); Hold Monster, Steel Wind Strike (9).
- **Guided Strike (Level 3):** Channel Divinity — give an attack roll a +10 bonus (Reaction to benefit an ally).
- **War Priest (Level 3):** Bonus Action — make one weapon or Unarmed Strike attack. Uses = Wisdom modifier (min 1); recharge on Short or Long Rest.
- **War God's Blessing (Level 6):** Channel Divinity to cast Shield of Faith or Spiritual Weapon without a spell slot (no Concentration; lasts 1 minute).
- **Avatar of Battle (Level 17):** Resistance to Bludgeoning, Piercing, and Slashing damage.
