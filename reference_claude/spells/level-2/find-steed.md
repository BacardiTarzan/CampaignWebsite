# FIND STEED
**Level:** 2
**School:** Conjuration
**Classes:** Paladin
**Casting Time:** Action
**Range:** 30 feet
**Components:** V, S
**Duration:** Instantaneous

You summon an otherworldly being that appears as a loyal steed in an unoccupied space of your choice within range. This creature uses the Otherworldly Steed stat block. If you already have a steed from this spell, the steed is replaced by the new one. The steed resembles a Large, rideable animal of your choice. Choose the steed's creature type — Celestial, Fey, or Fiend — which determines certain traits in its stat block.

The steed is an ally to you and your allies. In combat, it shares your Initiative count and functions as a controlled mount while you ride it. If you have the Incapacitated condition, the steed acts independently, focusing on protecting you.

The steed disappears if it drops to 0 Hit Points or if you die. If you cast this spell again, you decide whether you summon the steed that disappeared or a different one.

**Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot.** Use the spell slot's level for the spell's level in the stat block.
