# HALFLING
**Creature Type:** Humanoid
**Size:** Small (2–3 ft.)
**Speed:** 30 feet
**Lifespan:** Around 150 years

### Traits
**Brave:** Advantage on saves to avoid or end the Frightened condition.

**Halfling Nimbleness:** Can move through the space of any creature larger than you (cannot stop there).

**Luck:** When you roll a 1 on the d20 of a D20 Test, reroll the die and use the new roll.

**Naturally Stealthy:** Can take the Hide action even when obscured only by a creature at least one size larger than you.
