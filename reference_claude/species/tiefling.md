# TIEFLING
**Creature Type:** Humanoid
**Size:** Medium (4–7 ft.) or Small (3–4 ft.), chosen at character creation
**Speed:** 30 feet

### Traits
**Darkvision:** 60 feet.

**Fiendish Legacy:** Choose one legacy; gain its level 1 benefit and spells at levels 3 and 5 (always prepared; cast once without a slot per Long Rest; also castable with spell slots). Choose Intelligence, Wisdom, or Charisma as your spellcasting ability.

| Legacy | Level 1 | Level 3 Spell | Level 5 Spell |
|--------|---------|---------------|---------------|
| Abyssal | Resistance to Poison; know Poison Spray | Ray of Sickness | Hold Person |
| Chthonic | Resistance to Necrotic; know Chill Touch | False Life | Ray of Enfeeblement |
| Infernal | Resistance to Fire; know Fire Bolt | Hellish Rebuke | Darkness |

**Otherworldly Presence:** Know the Thaumaturgy cantrip (uses same spellcasting ability as Fiendish Legacy).
