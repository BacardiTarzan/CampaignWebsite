# DRAGONBORN
**Creature Type:** Humanoid
**Size:** Medium (5–7 ft.)
**Speed:** 30 feet

### Draconic Ancestry
| Dragon | Damage Type |
|--------|-------------|
| Black | Acid |
| Blue | Lightning |
| Brass | Fire |
| Bronze | Lightning |
| Copper | Acid |
| Gold | Fire |
| Green | Poison |
| Red | Fire |
| Silver | Cold |
| White | Cold |

### Traits
**Draconic Ancestry:** Choose a dragon type — determines your Breath Weapon damage type, Damage Resistance, and appearance.

**Breath Weapon:** When taking the Attack action, replace one attack with a 15-ft. Cone or 30-ft. Line (5 ft. wide). Creatures make a Dexterity save (DC 8 + Con modifier + Prof.) or take 1d10 damage (half on success). Scales: 2d10 at level 5, 3d10 at level 11, 4d10 at level 17. Uses = Proficiency Bonus; recharge on Long Rest.

**Damage Resistance:** Resistance to your Draconic Ancestry damage type.

**Darkvision:** 60 feet.

**Draconic Flight (Level 5):** Bonus Action — sprout spectral wings for 10 minutes (Fly Speed = Speed). Once per Long Rest.
