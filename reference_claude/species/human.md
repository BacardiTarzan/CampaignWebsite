# HUMAN
**Creature Type:** Humanoid
**Size:** Medium (4–7 ft.) or Small (2–4 ft.), chosen at character creation
**Speed:** 30 feet

### Traits
**Resourceful:** Gain Heroic Inspiration whenever you finish a Long Rest.

**Skillful:** Gain proficiency in one skill of your choice.

**Versatile:** Gain one Origin feat of your choice (Skilled recommended).
