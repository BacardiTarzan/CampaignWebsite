# GOLIATH
**Creature Type:** Humanoid
**Size:** Medium (7–8 ft.)
**Speed:** 35 feet

### Traits
**Giant Ancestry:** Choose one supernatural boon (uses = Proficiency Bonus; recharge on Long Rest):
- **Cloud's Jaunt (Cloud Giant):** Bonus Action — teleport up to 30 ft. to an unoccupied space you can see.
- **Fire's Burn (Fire Giant):** On a damaging hit, also deal 1d10 Fire damage.
- **Frost's Chill (Frost Giant):** On a damaging hit, also deal 1d6 Cold damage and reduce target's Speed by 10 ft. until your next turn.
- **Hill's Tumble (Hill Giant):** On a damaging hit vs. a Large or smaller creature, give it the Prone condition.
- **Stone's Endurance (Stone Giant):** Reaction when taking damage — roll 1d12 + Con modifier and reduce the damage by that amount.
- **Storm's Thunder (Storm Giant):** Reaction when damaged by a creature within 60 ft. — deal 1d8 Thunder damage to that creature.

**Large Form (Level 5):** Bonus Action — become Large for 10 minutes (Advantage on Strength checks, Speed +10 ft.). Once per Long Rest.

**Powerful Build:** Advantage on saves to end the Grappled condition. Count as one size larger for carrying capacity.
