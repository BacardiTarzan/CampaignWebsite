# ELF
**Creature Type:** Humanoid
**Size:** Medium (5–6 ft.)
**Speed:** 30 feet (Wood Elf: 35 feet)
**Lifespan:** Around 750 years

### Traits
**Darkvision:** 60 feet.

**Elven Lineage:** Choose one lineage; gain its level 1 benefit and spells at levels 3 and 5 (always prepared; cast once without a slot per Long Rest; also castable with spell slots). Choose Intelligence, Wisdom, or Charisma as your spellcasting ability.

| Lineage | Level 1 | Level 3 Spell | Level 5 Spell |
|---------|---------|---------------|---------------|
| Drow | Darkvision increases to 120 ft.; know Dancing Lights | Faerie Fire | Darkness |
| High Elf | Know Prestidigitation (replaceable on Long Rest with any Wizard cantrip) | Detect Magic | Misty Step |
| Wood Elf | Speed increases to 35 ft.; know Druidcraft | Longstrider | Pass without Trace |

**Fey Ancestry:** Advantage on saves to avoid or end the Charmed condition.

**Keen Senses:** Proficiency in Insight, Perception, or Survival (choose one).

**Trance:** No need to sleep; magic cannot put you to sleep. Long Rest takes only 4 hours of trancelike meditation.
