# GNOME
**Creature Type:** Humanoid
**Size:** Small (3–4 ft.)
**Speed:** 30 feet
**Lifespan:** Around 425 years

### Traits
**Darkvision:** 60 feet.

**Gnomish Cunning:** Advantage on Intelligence, Wisdom, and Charisma saving throws.

**Gnomish Lineage:** Choose one; select Intelligence, Wisdom, or Charisma as your spellcasting ability.
- **Forest Gnome:** Know Minor Illusion cantrip. Always have Speak with Animals prepared; cast without a slot (uses = Proficiency Bonus; recharge on Long Rest); also castable with spell slots.
- **Rock Gnome:** Know Mending and Prestidigitation cantrips. Can spend 10 minutes casting Prestidigitation to create a Tiny clockwork device (AC 5, 1 HP) that produces one chosen Prestidigitation effect when activated as a Bonus Action. Up to 3 devices can exist at once; each lasts 8 hours or until dismantled.
