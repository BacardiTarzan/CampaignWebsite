# DWARF
**Creature Type:** Humanoid
**Size:** Medium (4–5 ft.)
**Speed:** 30 feet
**Lifespan:** Around 350 years

### Traits
**Darkvision:** 120 feet.

**Dwarven Resilience:** Resistance to Poison damage; Advantage on saves to avoid or end the Poisoned condition.

**Dwarven Toughness:** HP maximum +1, and +1 again at each level gained.

**Stonecunning:** Bonus Action — gain Tremorsense 60 ft. for 10 minutes while on or touching a stone surface (natural or worked). Uses = Proficiency Bonus; recharge on Long Rest.
