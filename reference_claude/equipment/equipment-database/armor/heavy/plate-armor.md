# Plate Armor

**Type:** Heavy Armor  
**Armor Class (AC):** 18  
**Strength Requirement:** Strength 15  
**Weight:** 65 lb.  
**Cost:** 1,500 GP  
**Don/Doff Time:** 10 minutes to don, 5 minutes to doff

## Properties
- Disadvantage on Stealth checks

## Description
The most protective form of armor, made of heavy metal plates covering the entire body. Expensive, bulky, and requires great strength. The armor of choice for wealthy knights and elite soldiers.
