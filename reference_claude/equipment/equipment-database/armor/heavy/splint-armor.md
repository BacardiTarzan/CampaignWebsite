# Splint Armor

**Type:** Heavy Armor  
**Armor Class (AC):** 17  
**Strength Requirement:** Strength 15  
**Weight:** 60 lb.  
**Cost:** 200 GP  
**Don/Doff Time:** 10 minutes to don, 5 minutes to doff

## Properties
- Disadvantage on Stealth checks

## Description
Armor made of vertical metal bands reinforced with chain mail, providing excellent protection for those strong enough to wear it.
