# Chain Mail

**Type:** Heavy Armor  
**Armor Class (AC):** 16  
**Strength Requirement:** Strength 13  
**Weight:** 55 lb.  
**Cost:** 75 GP  
**Don/Doff Time:** 10 minutes to don, 5 minutes to doff

## Properties
- Disadvantage on Stealth checks

## Description
Interlinking metal rings forming complete body coverage. Requires significant strength to wear without penalty to movement.
