# Ring Mail

**Type:** Heavy Armor  
**Armor Class (AC):** 14  
**Strength Requirement:** —  
**Weight:** 40 lb.  
**Cost:** 30 GP  
**Don/Doff Time:** 10 minutes to don, 5 minutes to doff

## Properties
- Disadvantage on Stealth checks

## Description
Armor made from interlocking metal rings sewn to a backing, bulkier than chain mail but cheaper.
