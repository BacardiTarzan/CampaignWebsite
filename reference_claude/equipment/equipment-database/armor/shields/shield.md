# Shield

**Type:** Shield  
**Armor Class Bonus:** +2 AC  
**Weight:** 6 lb.  
**Cost:** 10 GP

## Description
A piece of protective equipment that grants a +2 bonus to your Armor Class when equipped. You can use only one shield at a time, and you must have training with shields to use them effectively.

## Shield Training
You gain the Armor Class benefit of a Shield only if you have training with it. If you wear armor and don't have training with it, you have Disadvantage on any D20 Test that involves Strength or Dexterity, and you can't cast spells.
