# Half Plate Armor

**Type:** Medium Armor  
**Armor Class (AC):** 15 + Dexterity modifier (max +2)  
**Strength Requirement:** —  
**Weight:** 40 lb.  
**Cost:** 750 GP  
**Don/Doff Time:** 5 minutes to don, 1 minute to doff

## Properties
- Disadvantage on Stealth checks

## Description
Plate armor covering the upper body and legs, providing excellent protection but heavier than breastplate alone.
