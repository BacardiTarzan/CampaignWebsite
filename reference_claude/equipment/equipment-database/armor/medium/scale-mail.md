# Scale Mail

**Type:** Medium Armor  
**Armor Class (AC):** 14 + Dexterity modifier (max +2)  
**Strength Requirement:** —  
**Weight:** 45 lb.  
**Cost:** 50 GP  
**Don/Doff Time:** 5 minutes to don, 1 minute to doff

## Properties
- Disadvantage on Stealth checks

## Description
Armor made from small metal scales overlapping like fish scales, providing excellent protection at the cost of being somewhat noisy.
