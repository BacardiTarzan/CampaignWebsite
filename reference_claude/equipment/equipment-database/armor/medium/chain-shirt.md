# Chain Shirt

**Type:** Medium Armor  
**Armor Class (AC):** 13 + Dexterity modifier (max +2)  
**Strength Requirement:** —  
**Weight:** 20 lb.  
**Cost:** 50 GP  
**Don/Doff Time:** 5 minutes to don, 1 minute to doff

## Properties
- None

## Description
A sleeveless shirt of interlinking metal rings, lighter than full chain mail but still protective. Popular with guards and soldiers.
