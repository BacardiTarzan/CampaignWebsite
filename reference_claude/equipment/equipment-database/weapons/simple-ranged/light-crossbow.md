# Light Crossbow

**Type:** Simple Ranged Weapon  
**Damage:** 1d8 Piercing  
**Weight:** 5 lb.  
**Cost:** 25 GP  
**Ammunition:** Bolts (Range 80/320)

## Properties
- Ammunition (Range 80/320; Bolt)
- Loading
- Two-Handed

## Mastery
- Slow

## Description
A crossbow sized for one hand to operate, though it still requires two hands to aim and fire effectively. Requires bolts as ammunition.
