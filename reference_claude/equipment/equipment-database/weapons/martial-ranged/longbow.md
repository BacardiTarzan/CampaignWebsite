# Longbow

**Type:** Martial Ranged Weapon  
**Damage:** 1d8 Piercing  
**Weight:** 2 lb.  
**Cost:** 50 GP  
**Ammunition:** Arrows (Range 150/600)

## Properties
- Ammunition (Range 150/600; Arrow)
- Heavy
- Two-Handed

## Mastery
- Slow

## Description
A large bow made of yew or similar strong wood, capable of firing arrows great distances. Requires proficiency and strength to use effectively.
