# Trident

**Type:** Martial Melee Weapon  
**Damage:** 1d8 Piercing (1d10 two-handed)  
**Weight:** 4 lb.  
**Cost:** 5 GP  

## Properties
- Thrown (Range 20/60)
- Versatile (1d10)

## Mastery
- Topple

## Description
A spear with three prongs, allowing it to be thrown or wielded with one or two hands. Popular with aquatic warriors.
