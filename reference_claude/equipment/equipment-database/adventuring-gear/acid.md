# Acid

**Cost:** 25 GP  
**Weight:** 1 lb.  

## Description
A vial of strong acid that can be thrown as a weapon or used to dissolve materials.

## Combat Use
When you take the Attack action, you can replace one of your attacks with throwing a vial of Acid. Target one creature or object you can see within 20 feet of yourself. The target must succeed on a Dexterity saving throw (DC 8 + your Dexterity modifier + Proficiency Bonus) or take 2d6 Acid damage.
