# Services

### Lifestyle Expenses

Choose a lifestyle at the start of each week or month and pay the price to sustain it.

| Lifestyle | Cost |
|-----------|------|
| Wretched | Free |
| Squalid | 1 SP per day |
| Poor | 2 SP per day |
| Modest | 1 GP per day |
| Comfortable | 2 GP per day |
| Wealthy | 4 GP per day |
| Aristocratic | 10 GP per day |
