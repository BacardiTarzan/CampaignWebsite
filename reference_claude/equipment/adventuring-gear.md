### Gear Descriptions

**Acid (25 GP).** When you take the Attack action, you can replace one of your attacks with throwing a vial of Acid. Target one creature or object you can see within 20 feet. The target must succeed on a Dexterity saving throw (DC 8 + your Dexterity modifier + Proficiency Bonus) or take 2d6 Acid damage.

**Alchemist's Fire (50 GP).** When you take the Attack action, you can replace one of your attacks with throwing a flask of Alchemist's Fire. Target one creature or object you can see within 20 feet. The target must succeed on a Dexterity saving throw (DC 8 + your Dexterity modifier + Proficiency Bonus) or take 1d4 Fire damage and start burning.

**Antitoxin (50 GP).** As a Bonus Action, you can drink a vial of Antitoxin to gain Advantage on saving throws to avoid or end the Poisoned condition for 1 hour.

**Backpack (2 GP).** Holds up to 30 pounds within 1 cubic foot. Can also serve as a saddlebag.

**Ball Bearings (1 GP).** As a Utilize action, you can spill Ball Bearings to cover a level, 10-foot-square area within 10 feet of yourself. A creature that enters this area for the first time on a turn must succeed on a DC 10 Dexterity saving throw or have the Prone condition. It takes 10 minutes to recover the Ball Bearings.

**Barrel (2 GP).** Holds up to 40 gallons of liquid or up to 4 cubic feet of dry goods.

**Bedroll (1 GP).** Sleeps one Small or Medium creature. While in a Bedroll, you automatically succeed on saving throws against extreme cold.

**Bell (1 GP).** When rung as a Utilize action, produces a sound audible up to 60 feet away.

**Blanket (5 SP).** While wrapped in a blanket, you have Advantage on saving throws against extreme cold.

**Block and Tackle (1 GP).** Allows you to hoist up to four times the weight you can normally lift.

**Book (25 GP).** If you consult an accurate nonfiction Book about its topic, you gain a +5 bonus to Intelligence (Arcana, History, Nature, or Religion) checks you make about that topic.

**Burglar's Pack (16 GP).** Contains: Backpack, Ball Bearings, Bell, 10 Candles, Crowbar, Hooded Lantern, 7 flasks of Oil, 5 days of Rations, Rope, Tinderbox, and Waterskin.

**Caltrops (1 GP).** As a Utilize action, you can spread Caltrops to cover a 5-foot-square area within 5 feet of yourself. A creature that enters this area for the first time on a turn must succeed on a DC 15 Dexterity saving throw or take 1 Piercing damage and have its Speed reduced to 0 until the start of its next turn. It takes 10 minutes to recover the Caltrops.

**Candle (1 CP).** For 1 hour, a lit Candle sheds Bright Light in a 5-foot radius and Dim Light for an additional 5 feet.

**Chain (5 GP).** As a Utilize action, you can wrap Chain around an unwilling creature within 5 feet that has the Grappled, Incapacitated, or Restrained condition if you succeed on a DC 13 Strength (Athletics) check. If the creature's legs are bound, it has the Restrained condition until it escapes. Escaping requires a DC 18 Dexterity (Acrobatics) check as an action. Bursting the Chain requires a DC 20 Strength (Athletics) check as an action.

**Climber's Kit (25 GP).** As a Utilize action, you can use the Climber's Kit to anchor yourself; when you do, you can't fall more than 25 feet from the anchor point, and you can't move more than 25 feet from there without undoing the anchor as a Bonus Action.

**Clothes, Fine (15 GP).** Some events and locations admit only people wearing Fine Clothes.

**Component Pouch (25 GP).** Holds all the free Material components of your spells.

**Costume (5 GP).** While wearing a Costume, you have Advantage on any ability check you make to impersonate the person or type of person it represents.

**Crowbar (2 GP).** Gives you Advantage on Strength checks where the Crowbar's leverage can be applied.

**Diplomat's Pack (39 GP).** Contains: Chest, Fine Clothes, Ink, 5 Ink Pens, Lamp, 2 Map or Scroll Cases, 4 flasks of Oil, 5 sheets of Paper, 5 sheets of Parchment, Perfume, and Tinderbox.

**Dungeoneer's Pack (12 GP).** Contains: Backpack, Caltrops, Crowbar, 2 flasks of Oil, 10 days of Rations, Rope, Tinderbox, 10 Torches, and Waterskin.

**Entertainer's Pack (40 GP).** Contains: Backpack, Bedroll, Bell, Bullseye Lantern, 3 Costumes, Mirror, 8 flasks of Oil, 9 days of Rations, Tinderbox, and Waterskin.

**Explorer's Pack (10 GP).** Contains: Backpack, Bedroll, 2 flasks of Oil, 10 days of Rations, Rope, Tinderbox, 10 Torches, and Waterskin.

**Grappling Hook (2 GP).** As a Utilize action, you can throw the Grappling Hook at a railing, ledge, or another catch within 50 feet. The hook catches on if you succeed on a DC 13 Dexterity (Acrobatics) check. If you tied a Rope to the hook, you can then climb it.

**Healer's Kit (5 GP).** Has ten uses. As a Utilize action, you can expend one use to stabilize an Unconscious creature that has 0 Hit Points without needing to make a Wisdom (Medicine) check.

**Holy Water (25 GP).** When you take the Attack action, you can replace one of your attacks with throwing a flask of Holy Water. Target one creature you can see within 20 feet. The target must succeed on a Dexterity saving throw (DC 8 + your Dexterity modifier + Proficiency Bonus) or take 2d8 Radiant damage if it is a Fiend or an Undead.

**Hunting Trap (5 GP).** As a Utilize action, you can set a Hunting Trap. A creature that steps on the plate must succeed on a DC 13 Dexterity saving throw or take 1d4 Piercing damage and have its Speed reduced to 0 until the start of its next turn. Thereafter, until the creature breaks free (DC 13 Strength (Athletics) check as an action; each failed check deals 1 Piercing damage), its movement is limited by the chain (typically 3 feet).

**Lamp (5 SP).** Burns Oil as fuel to cast Bright Light in a 15-foot radius and Dim Light for an additional 30 feet.

**Lantern, Bullseye (10 GP).** Burns Oil as fuel to cast Bright Light in a 60-foot Cone and Dim Light for an additional 60 feet.

**Lantern, Hooded (5 GP).** Burns Oil as fuel to cast Bright Light in a 30-foot radius and Dim Light for an additional 30 feet. As a Bonus Action, you can lower the hood, reducing the light to Dim Light in a 5-foot radius, or raise it again.

**Lock (10 GP).** Comes with a key. Without the key, a creature can use Thieves' Tools to pick this Lock with a successful DC 15 Dexterity (Sleight of Hand) check.

**Magnifying Glass (100 GP).** Grants Advantage on any ability check made to appraise or inspect a highly detailed item. Can also light a fire (requires bright sunlight, tinder, and about 5 minutes).

**Manacles (2 GP).** As a Utilize action, you can bind an unwilling Small or Medium creature within 5 feet that has the Grappled, Incapacitated, or Restrained condition if you succeed on a DC 13 Dexterity (Sleight of Hand) check. While bound, the creature has Disadvantage on attack rolls, and is Restrained if attached to a fixed chain or hook. Escaping requires a DC 20 Dexterity (Sleight of Hand) check as an action. Bursting requires a DC 25 Strength (Athletics) check as an action. Without the key, Thieves' Tools can pick the lock (DC 15 Dexterity (Sleight of Hand) check).

**Map (1 GP).** If you consult an accurate Map, you gain a +5 bonus to Wisdom (Survival) checks to find your way in the place represented on it.

**Net (1 GP).** When you take the Attack action, you can replace one of your attacks with throwing a Net at a creature you can see within 15 feet. The target must succeed on a Dexterity saving throw (DC 8 + your Dexterity modifier + Proficiency Bonus) or have the Restrained condition until it escapes. Huge or larger creatures succeed automatically. To escape, the target or a creature within 5 feet must make a DC 10 Strength (Athletics) check as an action. Destroying the Net (AC 10; 5 HP; Immunity to Bludgeoning, Poison, and Psychic damage) also frees the target.

**Oil (1 SP).** Three uses:
- **Dousing a Creature or Object.** As part of the Attack action (replacing one attack), target one creature or object within 20 feet (DC 8 + Dex modifier + PB). On a failed save, if the target takes Fire damage within 1 minute, it takes an extra 5 Fire damage from burning oil.
- **Dousing a Space.** As a Utilize action, pour Oil on a 5-foot-square area within 5 feet. If lit, it burns for 2 rounds and deals 5 Fire damage to any creature that enters the area or ends its turn there (once per turn).
- **Fuel.** One flask burns for 6 hours in a Lamp or Lantern (duration need not be consecutive).

**Perfume (5 GP).** For 1 hour after applying, you have Advantage on Charisma (Persuasion) checks made to influence an Indifferent Humanoid within 5 feet of yourself.

**Poison, Basic (100 GP).** As a Bonus Action, coat one weapon or up to three pieces of ammunition. A creature that takes Piercing or Slashing damage from the poisoned weapon or ammunition takes an extra 1d4 Poison damage. The poison retains potency for 1 minute or until its damage is dealt, whichever comes first.

**Pole (5 CP).** 10 feet long. You can use it to touch something up to 10 feet away. You can also vault with it for Advantage on Strength (Athletics) checks as part of a High or Long Jump.

**Portable Ram (4 GP).** Gives a +4 bonus to the Strength check to break down a door. One other character can help you use the ram, giving you Advantage on this check.

**Potion of Healing (50 GP).** Magic item. As a Bonus Action, you can drink it or administer it to another creature within 5 feet. The creature regains 2d4 + 2 Hit Points.

**Priest's Pack (33 GP).** Contains: Backpack, Blanket, Holy Water, Lamp, 7 days of Rations, Robe, and Tinderbox.

**Robe (1 GP).** Some events and locations admit only people wearing a Robe bearing certain colors or symbols.

**Rope (1 GP).** As a Utilize action, you can tie a knot (DC 10 Dexterity (Sleight of Hand) check). Can be burst with a DC 20 Strength (Athletics) check. You can bind an unwilling creature with the Rope only if it has the Grappled, Incapacitated, or Restrained condition. If the creature's legs are bound, it has the Restrained condition until it escapes (DC 15 Dexterity (Acrobatics) check as an action).

**Scholar's Pack (40 GP).** Contains: Backpack, Book, Ink, Ink Pen, Lamp, 10 flasks of Oil, 10 sheets of Parchment, and Tinderbox.

**Signal Whistle (5 CP).** When blown as a Utilize action, produces a sound audible up to 600 feet away.

**Spell Scroll (Cantrip, 30 GP; Level 1, 50 GP).** Magic item. If the spell is on your class's spell list, you can read the scroll and cast the spell using its normal casting time without providing any Material components. Spell save DC is 13; attack bonus is +5. The scroll disintegrates when the casting is completed.

**Spyglass (1,000 GP).** Objects viewed through a Spyglass are magnified to twice their size.

**Tinderbox (5 SP).** Lighting a Candle, Lamp, Lantern, or Torch takes a Bonus Action. Lighting any other fire takes 1 minute.

**Torch (1 CP).** Burns for 1 hour, casting Bright Light in a 20-foot radius and Dim Light for an additional 20 feet. Can be used as a Simple Melee weapon (Attack action); on a hit, deals 1 Fire damage.

**Waterskin (2 SP).** Holds up to 4 pints. If you don't drink sufficient water, you risk dehydration.
