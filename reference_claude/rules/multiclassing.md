# Multiclassing Rules

Multiclassing allows you to gain levels in multiple classes. When you advance in level, you may take a level in a new class instead of your current one.

---

## Prerequisites

To qualify for a new class, you must meet **both**:
- A score of at least 13 in your **current class's** primary ability
- A score of at least 13 in the **new class's** primary ability

| Class | Minimum Ability Score Required |
|-------|-------------------------------|
| Barbarian | Strength 13 |
| Bard | Charisma 13 |
| Cleric | Wisdom 13 |
| Druid | Wisdom 13 |
| Fighter | Strength 13 or Dexterity 13 |
| Monk | Dexterity 13 and Wisdom 13 |
| Paladin | Strength 13 and Charisma 13 |
| Ranger | Dexterity 13 and Wisdom 13 |
| Rogue | Dexterity 13 |
| Sorcerer | Charisma 13 |
| Warlock | Charisma 13 |
| Wizard | Intelligence 13 |

---

## Experience Points

XP cost is based on your **total character level**, not your level in any particular class. Use the Character Advancement table in `rules/character-creation.md`.

---

## Hit Points and Hit Dice

- You gain Hit Points from your new class as if you were gaining levels after level 1 (no max-die benefit — roll or use fixed value).
- **Level 1 Hit Points** (max die + Con) only apply when your total character level is 1.
- Add your new class's Hit Die to your pool. Track separately if the dice are different types.

---

## Proficiency Bonus

Based on **total character level**, not level in any specific class. See the Character Advancement table.

---

## Proficiencies Gained When Multiclassing

When you take your first level in a new class, you gain only **some** starting proficiencies — not the full first-level set. Each class description notes which proficiencies multiclassing characters gain.

### Multiclass Proficiency Grants by Class

| Class | Proficiencies Gained |
|-------|---------------------|
| Barbarian | Shields, Simple weapons, Martial weapons |
| Bard | Light armor, one skill of your choice, one Musical Instrument |
| Cleric | Light armor, Medium armor, Shields |
| Druid | Light armor, Medium armor, Shields (Druids won't wear metal armor) |
| Fighter | Light armor, Medium armor, Shields, Simple weapons, Martial weapons |
| Monk | Simple weapons |
| Paladin | Light armor, Medium armor, Shields, Simple weapons, Martial weapons |
| Ranger | Light armor, Medium armor, Shields, Simple weapons, Martial weapons, one skill from Ranger list |
| Rogue | Light armor, one skill from Rogue list, Thieves' Tools |
| Sorcerer | — |
| Warlock | Light armor, Simple weapons |
| Wizard | — |

---

## Class Features

You gain class features for each new level in a class normally — with these exceptions:

### Armor Class
If you have multiple base AC calculations (e.g., Monk's Unarmored Defense and Sorcerer's Draconic Resilience), you can benefit from only **one** at a time.

### Extra Attack
Extra Attack features from different classes **don't stack**. You can't make more than two attacks with this feature unless a specific feature says otherwise (e.g., Fighter's Two Extra Attacks).

---

## Spellcasting When Multiclassing

### Spells Prepared
Determine spells prepared **for each class separately**, as if you were a single-class member of that class. Each spell uses the spellcasting ability of its class.

### Cantrips
Cantrip power scales with your **total character level**, not your level in the granting class.

### Spell Slots (Combined Pool)
Calculate your combined spell slot level by adding:
- **All** levels in Bard, Cleric, Druid, Sorcerer, Wizard
- **Half** your levels (round up) in Paladin and Ranger
- **One-third** your levels (round down) in Fighter (Eldritch Knight subclass only) and Rogue (Arcane Trickster subclass only)

Look up this total on the **Multiclass Spellcaster table** in `rules/spell-slots.md`. These combined slots can be used for spells from any class whose Spellcasting feature you have.

**Example:** Level 4 Ranger / Level 3 Sorcerer = 2 (half of 4, round up) + 3 = 5. Use the level 5 row of the table.

**Example:** Level 6 Eldritch Knight Fighter / Level 2 Wizard = 2 (one-third of 6, round down) + 2 = 4. Use the level 4 row of the table.

### Warlock Pact Magic with Other Spellcasting
- Pact Magic slots can cast spells prepared from Spellcasting classes.
- Spellcasting slots can cast Warlock spells you have prepared.
- The two slot pools remain separate — they don't combine into the Multiclass table.

---

## Subclasses When Multiclassing

Subclass features require sufficient levels **in the specific class** — not total character level. A level 3 Barbarian / level 3 Ranger who wants their Ranger subclass must have 3 Ranger levels (which they do), but choosing the Barbarian subclass also requires 3 Barbarian levels (which they also have).
