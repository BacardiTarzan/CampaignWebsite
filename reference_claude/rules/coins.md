# Coins

| Coin | Value in GP |
|------|-------------|
| Copper Piece (CP) | 1/100 |
| Silver Piece (SP) | 1/10 |
| Electrum Piece (EP) | 1/2 |
| Gold Piece (GP) | 1 |
| Platinum Piece (PP) | 10 |

50 coins weigh 1 pound. Equipment sells for half its cost; trade goods and valuables retain their full value.
