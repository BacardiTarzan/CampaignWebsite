# DUAL WIELDER
**Category:** General
**Prerequisite:** Level 4+, Strength or Dexterity 13+

**Ability Score Increase:** +1 Strength or Dexterity (max 20).
**Enhanced Dual Wielding:** When you Attack with a Light weapon, make one extra Bonus Action attack with a different Melee weapon (not Two-Handed). No ability modifier to damage unless negative.
**Quick Draw:** Draw or stow two non-Two-Handed weapons when you'd normally draw one.
