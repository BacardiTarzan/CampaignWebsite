# SPEEDY
**Category:** General
**Prerequisite:** Level 4+, Dexterity or Constitution 13+

**Ability Score Increase:** +1 Dexterity or Constitution (max 20).
**Speed Increase:** Speed +10 feet.
**Dash over Difficult Terrain:** Difficult Terrain costs no extra movement during a Dash.
**Agile Movement:** Opportunity Attacks against you have Disadvantage.
