# SPELL SNIPER
**Category:** General
**Prerequisite:** Level 4+, Spellcasting or Pact Magic Feature

**Ability Score Increase:** +1 Intelligence, Wisdom, or Charisma (max 20).
**Bypass Cover:** Spell attack rolls ignore Half and Three-Quarters Cover.
**Casting in Melee:** No Disadvantage on spell attack rolls within 5 feet of an enemy.
**Increased Range:** Spells with range 10+ feet requiring an attack roll gain +60 feet of range.
