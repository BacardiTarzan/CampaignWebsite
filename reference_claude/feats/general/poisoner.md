# POISONER
**Category:** General
**Prerequisite:** Level 4+

**Ability Score Increase:** +1 Dexterity or Intelligence (max 20).
**Potent Poison:** Your Poison damage ignores Resistance to Poison.
**Brew Poison:** Proficiency with Poisoner's Kit. With 1 hour and 50 GP of materials, brew doses equal to your Proficiency Bonus. Bonus Action — apply a dose to a weapon or ammunition (lasts 1 minute or until you hit). Target makes a Con save (DC 8 + increased ability mod + PB) or takes 2d8 Poison damage and is Poisoned until the end of your next turn.
