# CRUSHER
**Category:** General
**Prerequisite:** Level 4+

**Ability Score Increase:** +1 Strength or Constitution (max 20).
**Push:** Once per turn, when you hit with Bludgeoning damage, move the target 5 feet to an unoccupied space (no more than one size larger).
**Enhanced Critical:** On a Bludgeoning Critical Hit, attack rolls against that creature have Advantage until your next turn.
