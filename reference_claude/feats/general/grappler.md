# GRAPPLER
**Category:** General
**Prerequisite:** Level 4+, Strength or Dexterity 13+

**Ability Score Increase:** +1 Strength or Dexterity (max 20).
**Punch and Grab:** Once per turn, when you hit with an Unarmed Strike during the Attack action, use both Damage and Grapple options.
**Attack Advantage:** Advantage on attack rolls against a creature you've Grappled.
**Fast Wrestler:** Speed isn't halved when moving a Grappled creature your size or smaller.
