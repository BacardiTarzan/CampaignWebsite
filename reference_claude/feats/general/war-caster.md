# WAR CASTER
**Category:** General
**Prerequisite:** Level 4+, Spellcasting or Pact Magic Feature

**Ability Score Increase:** +1 Intelligence, Wisdom, or Charisma (max 20).
**Concentration:** Advantage on Concentration saving throws.
**Reactive Spell:** When a creature provokes an Opportunity Attack by leaving your reach, cast a spell at it instead (1-action casting time, single target).
**Somatic Components:** Perform Somatic components even with weapons or a Shield in hand.
