# GREAT WEAPON MASTER
**Category:** General
**Prerequisite:** Level 4+, Strength 13+

**Ability Score Increase:** +1 Strength (max 20).
**Heavy Weapon Mastery:** When you hit with a Heavy weapon during the Attack action, deal extra damage equal to your Proficiency Bonus.
**Hew:** After a Critical Hit with a Melee weapon, or reducing a creature to 0 HP with one, make one Bonus Action attack with the same weapon.
