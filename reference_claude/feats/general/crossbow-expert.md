# CROSSBOW EXPERT
**Category:** General
**Prerequisite:** Level 4+, Dexterity 13+

**Ability Score Increase:** +1 Dexterity (max 20).
**Ignore Loading:** Ignore the Loading property on Hand, Heavy, and Light Crossbows. Can load without a free hand if holding the crossbow.
**Firing in Melee:** No Disadvantage on crossbow attacks within 5 feet of an enemy.
**Dual Wielding:** Add ability modifier to the Light property's extra attack damage when made with a Light crossbow.
