# TELEPATHIC
**Category:** General
**Prerequisite:** Level 4+

**Ability Score Increase:** +1 Intelligence, Wisdom, or Charisma (max 20).
**Telepathic Utterance:** Speak telepathically to a creature within 60 feet (one-way; uses a language you know).
**Detect Thoughts:** Always have *Detect Thoughts* prepared. Cast once per Long Rest without slot or components, or with available slots. Spellcasting ability is the increased ability.
