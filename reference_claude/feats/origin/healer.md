# HEALER
**Category:** Origin

**Battle Medic:** With a Healer's Kit, expend one use as a Utilize action to tend a creature within 5 feet. That creature spends one Hit Point Die; you roll it and they regain HP equal to the roll + your Proficiency Bonus.
**Healing Rerolls:** Reroll any 1 on healing dice from spells or Battle Medic; must use the new roll.
