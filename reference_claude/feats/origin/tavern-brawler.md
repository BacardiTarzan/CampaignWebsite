# TAVERN BRAWLER
**Category:** Origin

**Enhanced Unarmed Strike:** Your Unarmed Strike deals 1d4 + Strength modifier Bludgeoning damage.
**Damage Rerolls:** Reroll any 1 on Unarmed Strike damage; must use the new roll.
**Improvised Weaponry:** Proficiency with improvised weapons.
**Push:** Once per turn, when you hit with an Unarmed Strike during the Attack action, push the target 5 feet away.
