# MAGIC INITIATE
**Category:** Origin
**Repeatable:** Yes (different spell list each time)

**Two Cantrips:** Learn two cantrips from the Cleric, Druid, or Wizard spell list. Spellcasting ability is Int, Wis, or Cha (chosen at selection).
**Level 1 Spell:** Choose a level 1 spell from the same list. Always prepared. Cast once per Long Rest without a slot, or with any available spell slot.
**Spell Change:** When you gain a level, you can replace one chosen spell with another of the same level from the chosen list.
