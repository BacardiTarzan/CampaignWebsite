# BOON OF SPELL RECALL
**Category:** Epic Boon
**Prerequisite:** Level 19+, Spellcasting Feature

**Ability Score Increase:** +1 Intelligence, Wisdom, or Charisma (max 30).
**Free Casting:** When you cast a spell with a level 1–4 slot, roll 1d4. If the result equals the slot's level, the slot isn't expended.
