# BOON OF THE NIGHT SPIRIT
**Category:** Epic Boon
**Prerequisite:** Level 19+

**Ability Score Increase:** +1 to one ability score (max 30).
**Merge with Shadows:** In Dim Light or Darkness, Bonus Action — become Invisible until you take an action, Bonus Action, or Reaction.
**Shadowy Form:** In Dim Light or Darkness, Resistance to all damage except Psychic and Radiant.
