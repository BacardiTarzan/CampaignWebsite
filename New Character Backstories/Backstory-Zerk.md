# Zerk Armstrong — Backstory

*A handout for your character. Adjust as you like — this is yours.*

## Where You Come From

You grew up in a small goliath community — twenty, thirty people at most — tucked into hill country far from any human settlement. Your people had escaped the giants generations ago, but the freedom was still new enough that the elders spoke of it carefully, like something that could still be taken back.

When you were still young, the leaders of your community left in search of better ground in the high mountains. They said they would return when they had found a place worth moving to. They never came back. The few of you left behind held on for a while, then scattered. Each of you went your own way, looking for whatever felt like the right next thing.

You have wandered since. You've worked as a laborer, a guard, occasionally a fighter for hire. You have the strength for any of it. You don't talk much about the people you grew up with. You don't know if any of them are still alive.

## How You Met Dak

You met **Dak Roland** on the road some time ago. He was being ambushed by a group of bandits who'd misjudged his size and his anger. You happened to be passing through. You stepped in. By the time it was over, Dak had cut down three of them and you'd broken the necks of two more, and the rest had run.

He bought you a drink. You bought him three. You've ridden together ever since.

You are very different people. Dak looks for fights. Fights just seem to find you. Dak runs hot. You run steady. But he is, somehow, the closest friend you've ever had, and you trust him with your back without needing to think about it. He trusts you with his.

## How You Ended Up in Holmlette

A few weeks ago, you and Dak took caravan-guard work between **Aldwud Keep** and a town west. Bandits hit the caravan. The fight was bad. Dak put down two of them in a way the witness called "indistinguishable from murder." The magistrate told you both not to come back.

So you came to **Holmlette**. Small, quiet, no one asks questions. **Reeve Aldric** has been giving you and Dak odd jobs in exchange for room, board, and small coin. You haul, you patrol, you walk the fence line at night. It's not bad work. It's not enough work.

You've made one friend here: **Tomas the smith**. Tomas drinks too much and grieves the brother he lost to the wood last week. Now you sit with him most nights. You don't say much. He doesn't either. Although you've known him a short time, his loss sits heavy on you. You know all too well what it feels like to lose what family you have.

## Where You Are Now

You're tired of being broke, but you don't actually want to leave Holmlette. You like Tomas. You like the rhythm of the village. You like that no one here looks at you like you're a problem to be solved. But the trouble in the wood is getting worse, and Tomas's brother is one of the people missing, and Reeve Aldric is paying for someone to look into it.

You haven't said yes yet. But that has more to do with your aversion to talking than your willingness to take action.

## Hooks for You to Build On

* **The leaders who never came back.** Do you think about them? Do you blame them for leaving, or do you understand it? 
* **Your community's lost ways.** What did you learn from them before they scattered? A song, a craft, a saying, a particular way of cooking something? Pick one.
* **Why you stepped in for Dak.** Was it instinct? Did you see something in the fight that mattered? Was it the kind of thing you'd do for anyone?
* **What you carry.** You have a battleaxe. Is there anything else on your person that matters? A tooth, a stone from a place you remember, a bit of cloth from someone gone? Decide.
* **Tomas.** What is it about him that has made you want to stay?
