# Blistera — Backstory

*A handout for your character. Adjust as you like — this is yours. Everything here is a starting point; you're free to keep, change, or replace any of it.*

## Where You Come From

You are a forest gnome from a small enclave hidden in hill country a long week's walk west of Aldermark. You don't remember a great deal about it. When you were small, your family vanished — you have never gotten a satisfying answer to why or how, and the elders of your enclave handed you over to a passing healer of the Order of the Hearth-Mother soon after. You were raised in the order's mother-house from then on.

## The Order of the Hearth-Mother

The Hearth-Mother is a quiet, motherly goddess of mercy, hearths, and the small kept lights that hold back the dark. She is not famous. Her temples are not grand. Her priests serve villages too small for any larger faith — they treat the sick, deliver babies, bless the dead, and, where they can, teach reading.

You trained as both a **chirurgeon** (medicine, herbs, the practical care of bodies) and a **scribe** (letters, languages, the copying of old texts). Your order keeps a small library, and you spent a great deal of your youth in it. You read better than most country priests, and you can read more than one script.

That second part may matter sooner than you think.

## Why You're Here

You left the order's mother-house two years ago to do **circuit work** — traveling between villages too small to have their own healer or priest. You treat fevers. You deliver babies. You bless hearths. You read and write letters for those who can't. You go where you're needed and stay until you aren't.

You arrived in **Holmlette** two nights ago. You found **Father Beldwin** near the end of his rope — a tainted well, a sick child, a goat dead in the river, and now a feeling among the villagers that something is wrong they cannot name. You have been treating sick villagers since you arrived. You are tired.

Then a woman named Wynna brought a dying man in this morning, and things are about to get worse.

## What You Carry

You carry a healer's kit, a mace and shield (your order trains its members to defend themselves — the world is not always kind to small women on country roads), and the holy symbol of the Hearth-Mother: a small clay lamp, never lit, kept on a leather cord.

You also carry your scribe's tools and a small journal. You write in it most evenings. Reading old scripts is a particular love of yours.

## Hooks for You to Build On

* **Your family.** You don't know what happened to them. You may or may not want to know. That thread is open if you want to pull it.
* **A teacher.** Someone in the order's mother-house shaped you. Pick one — a name, a face, a way of saying things you can still hear. They are out there. You can write to them.
* **What you believe.** The Hearth-Mother is not a god of fire and judgment. She is a god of warmth and kept lights. What does that mean to *you*? Why this god, of all the gods?
* **A book you carry.** You have one book, beyond your journal, that you carry with you. What is it? A medical text? An old religious work? A copy of something you weren't strictly supposed to take from the library? Decide.
* **Why circuit work?** Your order has comfortable posts in the larger cities. You chose the road. Why? Restlessness? A sense of duty? Something you were running from?
