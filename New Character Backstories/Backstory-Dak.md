# Dak Roland — Backstory

*A handout for your character. Adjust as you like — this is yours.*

## What You Know About Yourself

You're a dragonborn fighter, and you don't apologize for being good at it. You came up in a tribal community before things got complicated and you went your own way. Since then, you've worked as a mercenary, a caravan guard, a road-warden — whatever pays. You've fought in a half-dozen scraps that ended worse for the other side. You're proud of that and don't pretend otherwise.

You met **Zerk Armstrong** on the road some time ago. He saved your life from an ambush, and you've ridden together since. He's the closest thing you have to a brother. You disagree about most things — Zerk is steadier than you, more patient, less interested in finishing a fight by killing everyone in it — but you trust him with your back, and he trusts you with his.

## How You Ended Up in Holmlette

Three or four weeks ago, you and Zerk were hired as caravan guards on a run between **Aldwud Keep** and a larger town a few days' ride west. Standard work. Standard pay.

The caravan was hit by bandits on the road. You fought hard. The merchant survived but lost a wagon's worth of goods. In the chaos, you put down two bandits in a way that one surviving witness described to the local magistrate as "indistinguishable from murder."

You say it was self-defense. The witness was scared. The magistrate didn't dig too hard.

The merchant didn't press charges, but the magistrate told you and Zerk not to come back. You drifted to **Holmlette** — small, quiet, no one cares who you are if you can swing a weapon. **Reeve Aldric** took you on for odd jobs: hauling, fence work, walking the perimeter on nights when the wolves had been bold. Room above the inn, food, a few coppers. Enough to stay quiet for a while.

You've been here three weeks. You have made exactly one friend: **Tomas the smith**, who drinks like you do and doesn't ask questions. His brother **Bart** went into the wood last week and didn't come back, and Tomas hasn't been right since.

## Where You Are Now

You're broke. You started with very little, and three weeks in a village without proper work has not improved that. You and Zerk are about ready to move on — except now there's trouble in Holmlette, and Reeve Aldric is the only one in the village offering paid work. You can't quite tell whether you're more interested in the gold or in the fact that Tomas's brother is one of the people missing.

You haven't decided yet how much of yourself you want to put into this.

## Hooks for You to Build On

* **Your tribe.** What was its name? Where is it now? Are you welcome there if you go back?
* **Why "indistinguishable from murder"?** What actually happened on that caravan road? You know. The witness may not have been wrong. You may never tell anyone the whole truth — or you may, at the right moment.
* **Your weapons.** The greatsword and the flail. One of them has a story. Pick one and decide what it is.
* **Zerk.** What does he really mean to you? A friend, a brother, a comrade? Or just a partner that helps bring around some paydays?
