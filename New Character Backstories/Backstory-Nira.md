# Nira Brackenheart — Backstory



*A handout for your character. Adjust as you like — this is yours.*



*Name Change: Didlybits could be a handle a halfling or gnome may appreciate, but Wood Elves tend to have surnames that reflect their family, enclave, or even accomplishments (many young Wood Elves go merely by their first name and 'earn' a surname more like a title). Brackenheart is a place holder, and even if you take it you should think about its importance. Why are you named that? Nira was probably to just name you, your surname describes you. 
Some ideas and suggestions:
family name - Greenleaf, Talloak, Summernight. Is this a translation from Elven to common? Do you have a 'true' last name you wouldn't share with outsiders?
title - Strongbow, Tree-Friend, Gold-Heart, True-Arrow. Did Nira do something in her life that gained her status or recognition from the enclave? If so, what?
Enclave - Greenshade (or you can change the name of the enclave) to designate where you are from. Maybe every elf in your enclave does this, and maybe they still gain titles when earned (Nira Swift-Knock Greenshade, Nira the True-Hearted of Greenshade, etc)*

### **Your Enclave**

You are a wood elf of the **Greenshade enclave**, a small community of perhaps forty elves living deep in the southern Aldwud. Your people have lived in this stretch of forest for longer than any village in Aldermark has existed. Your home is a cluster of woven longhouses and tree-shelters around a clearing the elders call *the Hearth*, where the ashes of a fire kept for centuries are tended in turn by whoever is home.

The Greenshade enclave is friendly with the human villages that ring the western edge of the wood — Holmlette especially. Your people trade pelts, herbs, and the occasional bit of tracking work for grain, iron tools, and worked cloth. The relationship is generations old. Your grandmother knew ten generations of the ancestors of Holmlette's current reeve.

## Your Place

You are young by elven standards but have been doing scouting and message-running for the enclave for several years. You have walked to Holmlette twice before this trip — once with your aunt as a guide, once alone — and you know the road. You have eaten in Gertha's inn. You know Reeve Aldric on sight, though you have only spoken to him a few times.

You also have a particular talent: you speak Orc. As a child, your enclave shared its eastern range with a small orc tribe, and you grew up playing with their children. You haven't seen any of them in years, but the language stuck.

## Your Errand

The Greenshade elders sent you to Holmlette on a routine check-in: visit, ask after the people the enclave knows, see if anything is wanted. This is normal work for someone of your standing. It was supposed to be a quiet trip — three days walking, two days in the village, three days back.

You arrived this morning.

Something is wrong in Holmlette. People you know by name will not meet your eye. There is a hostility in the air you have never felt here before. The villagers seem to think *the wood* has turned against them — and you, being of the wood, are tangled in that suspicion before you've spoken a word.

You don't know what's happened yet. You will need to figure it out — for your enclave, for the villagers you have always considered friends, and because something is very clearly wrong, and your people will want to know.

## Hooks for You to Build On

* **Your enclave.** Who back home matters most to you? A teacher, a sibling, a friend?
* **Your orc childhood friends.** What were their names? Where did they go? You may never see them again — or you may. Is there a different reason you speak the language?
* **A small belonging.** What is the one thing you would not lose? A knife from your father, a feather you found as a child, a small carving from someone you loved? Carry it on you.
