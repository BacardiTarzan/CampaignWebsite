# SLASHER
**Category:** General
**Prerequisite:** Level 4+

**Ability Score Increase:** +1 Strength or Dexterity (max 20).
**Hamstring:** Once per turn, when you hit with Slashing damage, reduce target's Speed by 10 feet until your next turn.
**Enhanced Critical:** On a Slashing Critical Hit, target has Disadvantage on attack rolls until your next turn.
