# CHARGER
**Category:** General
**Prerequisite:** Level 4+, Strength or Dexterity 13+

**Ability Score Increase:** +1 Strength or Dexterity (max 20).
**Improved Dash:** Speed +10 feet during a Dash action.
**Charge Attack:** After moving 10+ feet in a straight line toward a target and hitting with a melee attack during the Attack action, choose: +1d8 damage, OR push the target up to 10 feet (target no more than one size larger). Once per turn.
