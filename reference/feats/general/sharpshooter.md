# SHARPSHOOTER
**Category:** General
**Prerequisite:** Level 4+, Dexterity 13+

**Ability Score Increase:** +1 Dexterity (max 20).
**Bypass Cover:** Ranged weapon attacks ignore Half and Three-Quarters Cover.
**Firing in Melee:** No Disadvantage on Ranged weapon attacks within 5 feet of an enemy.
**Long Shots:** No Disadvantage at long range with Ranged weapons.
