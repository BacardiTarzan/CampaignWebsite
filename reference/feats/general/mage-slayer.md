# MAGE SLAYER
**Category:** General
**Prerequisite:** Level 4+

**Ability Score Increase:** +1 Strength or Dexterity (max 20).
**Concentration Breaker:** Damaged creatures have Disadvantage on Concentration saves.
**Guarded Mind:** Once per Short or Long Rest, turn a failed Int, Wis, or Cha save into a success.
