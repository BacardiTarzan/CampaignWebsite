# ELEMENTAL ADEPT
**Category:** General
**Prerequisite:** Level 4+, Spellcasting or Pact Magic Feature
**Repeatable:** Yes (different damage type each time)

**Ability Score Increase:** +1 Intelligence, Wisdom, or Charisma (max 20).
**Energy Mastery:** Choose Acid, Cold, Fire, Lightning, or Thunder. Your spells ignore Resistance to that damage type, and treat any 1 on a damage die as a 2.
