# ATHLETE
**Category:** General
**Prerequisite:** Level 4+, Strength or Dexterity 13+

**Ability Score Increase:** +1 Strength or Dexterity (max 20).
**Climb Speed:** Equal to your Speed.
**Hop Up:** Stand from Prone with only 5 feet of movement.
**Jumping:** Make a running Long or High Jump after only 5 feet of movement.
