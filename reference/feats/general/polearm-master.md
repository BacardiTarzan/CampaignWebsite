# POLEARM MASTER
**Category:** General
**Prerequisite:** Level 4+, Strength or Dexterity 13+

**Ability Score Increase:** +1 Dexterity or Strength (max 20).
**Pole Strike:** After Attacking with a Quarterstaff, Spear, or weapon with Heavy and Reach, use a Bonus Action to make a melee attack with the opposite end (1d4 Bludgeoning).
**Reactive Strike:** While holding such a weapon, Reaction to make one melee attack against a creature entering your reach.
