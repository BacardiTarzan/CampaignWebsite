# TELEKINETIC
**Category:** General
**Prerequisite:** Level 4+

**Ability Score Increase:** +1 Intelligence, Wisdom, or Charisma (max 20).
**Minor Telekinesis:** Learn *Mage Hand*. Cast without Verbal/Somatic components, can make the hand Invisible, +30 feet range. Spellcasting ability is the increased ability.
**Telekinetic Shove:** Bonus Action — target a creature within 30 feet. Strength save (DC 8 + increased ability mod + PB) or be moved 5 feet toward or away from you.
