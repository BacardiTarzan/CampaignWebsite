# INSPIRING LEADER
**Category:** General
**Prerequisite:** Level 4+, Wisdom or Charisma 13+

**Ability Score Increase:** +1 Wisdom or Charisma (max 20).
**Bolstering Performance:** After a Short or Long Rest, give a speech/song/dance. Up to six allies (including yourself) within 30 feet who witness it gain Temporary HP equal to your character level + the increased ability's modifier.
