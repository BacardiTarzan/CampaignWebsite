# SENTINEL
**Category:** General
**Prerequisite:** Level 4+, Strength or Dexterity 13+

**Ability Score Increase:** +1 Strength or Dexterity (max 20).
**Guardian:** Make an Opportunity Attack when a creature within 5 feet Disengages or hits a target other than you.
**Halt:** A creature hit by your Opportunity Attack has Speed 0 for the rest of that turn.
