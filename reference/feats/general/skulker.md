# SKULKER
**Category:** General
**Prerequisite:** Level 4+, Dexterity 13+

**Ability Score Increase:** +1 Dexterity (max 20).
**Blindsight:** 10-foot range.
**Fog of War:** Advantage on Dex (Stealth) checks made as part of the Hide action during combat.
**Sniper:** A missed attack roll while hidden doesn't reveal your location.
