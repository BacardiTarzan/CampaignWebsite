# ACTOR
**Category:** General
**Prerequisite:** Level 4+, Charisma 13+

**Ability Score Increase:** +1 Charisma (max 20).
**Impersonation:** Advantage on Cha (Deception or Performance) checks while disguised as a real or fictional person.
**Mimicry:** Mimic creature sounds and speech. DC to detect = 8 + Cha modifier + Proficiency Bonus (Wisdom [Insight] check).
