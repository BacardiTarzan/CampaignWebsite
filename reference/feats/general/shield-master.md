# SHIELD MASTER
**Category:** General
**Prerequisite:** Level 4+, Shield Training

**Ability Score Increase:** +1 Strength (max 20).
**Shield Bash:** Once per turn, after hitting with a Melee weapon during the Attack action, force a Strength save (DC 8 + Str mod + PB). On a failure, push 5 feet OR knock Prone (your choice).
**Interpose Shield:** Reaction — on a Dex save vs. half damage, take no damage on a success while holding a Shield.
