# MOUNTED COMBATANT
**Category:** General
**Prerequisite:** Level 4+

**Ability Score Increase:** +1 Strength, Dexterity, or Wisdom (max 20).
**Mounted Strike:** Advantage on attack rolls against unmounted creatures within 5 feet of your mount that are at least one size smaller than the mount.
**Leap Aside:** Mount takes no damage on a successful Dex save (instead of half), half on a failure. Requires you to be riding and neither Incapacitated.
**Veer:** Force an attack hitting your mount to hit you instead (must not be Incapacitated).
