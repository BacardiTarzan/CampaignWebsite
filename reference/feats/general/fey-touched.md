# FEY-TOUCHED
**Category:** General
**Prerequisite:** Level 4+

**Ability Score Increase:** +1 Intelligence, Wisdom, or Charisma (max 20).
**Fey Magic:** Choose one level 1 Divination or Enchantment spell. Always have it and *Misty Step* prepared. Cast each once per Long Rest without a slot, or with available slots. Spellcasting ability is the increased ability.
