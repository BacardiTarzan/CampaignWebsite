# RITUAL CASTER
**Category:** General
**Prerequisite:** Level 4+, Intelligence, Wisdom, or Charisma 13+

**Ability Score Increase:** +1 Intelligence, Wisdom, or Charisma (max 20).
**Ritual Spells:** Choose level 1 Ritual spells equal to your Proficiency Bonus. Always prepared; cast with any spell slot. Spellcasting ability is the increased ability. Add another level 1 Ritual spell each time PB increases.
**Quick Ritual:** Cast a prepared Ritual spell at its normal casting time without a spell slot. Once per Long Rest.
