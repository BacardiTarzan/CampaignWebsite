# CHEF
**Category:** General
**Prerequisite:** Level 4+

**Ability Score Increase:** +1 Constitution or Wisdom (max 20).
**Cook's Utensils:** Proficiency if you don't already have it.
**Replenishing Meal:** During a Short Rest, cook food for 4 + Proficiency Bonus creatures. Anyone eating who spends Hit Dice during the rest regains an extra 1d8 HP.
**Bolstering Treats:** With 1 hour of work or after a Long Rest, cook treats equal to your Proficiency Bonus (last 8 hours). A creature can use a Bonus Action to eat one and gain Temporary HP equal to your Proficiency Bonus.
