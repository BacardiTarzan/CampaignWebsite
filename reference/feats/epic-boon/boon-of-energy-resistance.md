# BOON OF ENERGY RESISTANCE
**Category:** Epic Boon
**Prerequisite:** Level 19+

**Ability Score Increase:** +1 to one ability score (max 30).
**Energy Resistances:** Resistance to two of: Acid, Cold, Fire, Lightning, Necrotic, Poison, Psychic, Radiant, or Thunder. Change choices on a Long Rest.
**Energy Redirection:** Reaction — when you take damage of a chosen type, redirect that damage type at a creature within 60 feet (not behind Total Cover). Dex save (DC 8 + Con mod + PB) or take 2d12 + Con modifier.
