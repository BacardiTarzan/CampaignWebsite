# BOON OF IRRESISTIBLE OFFENSE
**Category:** Epic Boon
**Prerequisite:** Level 19+

**Ability Score Increase:** +1 Strength or Dexterity (max 30).
**Overcome Defenses:** Your Bludgeoning, Piercing, and Slashing damage ignores Resistance.
**Overwhelming Strike:** When you roll a natural 20 on an attack roll, deal extra damage equal to the increased ability score (same damage type as the attack).
