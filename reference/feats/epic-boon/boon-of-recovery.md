# BOON OF RECOVERY
**Category:** Epic Boon
**Prerequisite:** Level 19+

**Ability Score Increase:** +1 to one ability score (max 30).
**Last Stand:** When reduced to 0 HP, drop to 1 HP instead and regain HP equal to half your maximum. Once per Long Rest.
**Recover Vitality:** Pool of ten d10s. Bonus Action — expend any number, roll, regain HP equal to total. Refreshes on a Long Rest.
