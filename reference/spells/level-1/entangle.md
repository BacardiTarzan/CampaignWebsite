# ENTANGLE
**Level:** 1
**School:** Conjuration
**Classes:** Druid, Ranger
**Casting Time:** Action
**Range:** 90 feet
**Components:** V, S
**Duration:** Concentration, up to 1 minute

Grasping plants sprout from the ground in a 20-foot square within range, turning the ground in the area into Difficult Terrain for the duration. Each creature (other than you) in the area when you cast the spell must succeed on a Strength saving throw or have the Restrained condition until the spell ends. A Restrained creature can take an action to make a Strength (Athletics) check against your spell save DC to free itself.
