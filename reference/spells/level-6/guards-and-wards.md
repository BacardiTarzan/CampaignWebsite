# GUARDS AND WARDS
**Level:** 6
**School:** Abjuration
**Classes:** Bard, Wizard
**Casting Time:** 1 hour
**Range:** Touch
**Components:** V, S, M (a silver rod worth 10+ GP)
**Duration:** 24 hours

You create a ward that protects up to 2,500 square feet of floor space. The warded area can be up to 20 feet tall, and you shape it as one 50-foot square, one hundred 5-foot squares that are contiguous, or twenty-five 10-foot squares that are contiguous.

You can specify individuals that are unaffected by the spell's effects. You can also specify a password that, when spoken aloud within 5 feet of the warded area, makes the speaker immune to its effects.

The spell creates the following effects: **Corridors** — fog fills all warded corridors, making them Heavily Obscured, and at each intersection there is a 50 percent chance a creature believes it is going in the opposite direction. **Doors** — all doors in the warded area are magically locked as if sealed by Arcane Lock; up to ten doors can be covered with an illusion making them appear as plain wall sections. **Stairs** — webs fill all stairs from top to bottom, as in the Web spell. **Other Spell Effect** — place one magical effect of your choice within the warded area, such as Dancing Lights, Magic Mouth, Stinking Cloud, Gust of Wind, or Suggestion in designated locations.
