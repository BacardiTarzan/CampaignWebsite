## GLOSSARY ENTRIES

---

### ABILITY CHECK

A D20 Test that represents using one of the six abilities — or a specific skill associated with an ability — to overcome a challenge.

---

### ABILITY SCORE AND MODIFIER

A creature has six ability scores: Strength, Dexterity, Constitution, Intelligence, Wisdom, and Charisma. Each has a corresponding modifier, added when making a D20 Test with that ability.

---

### ACTION

On your turn, you take one action — either from the standard list below or from a feature. Each is defined elsewhere in this glossary.

**Standard Actions:** Attack, Dash, Disengage, Dodge, Help, Hide, Influence, Magic, Ready, Search, Study, Utilize.

---

### ADVANTAGE

Roll two d20s and use the higher roll. A roll cannot have more than one Advantage; Advantage and Disadvantage on the same roll cancel out.

---

### ADVENTURE

A series of encounters; a story emerges through playing them.

---

### ALIGNMENT

Broadly describes a creature's ethical attitudes and ideals: a morality axis (good, evil, neutral) combined with an order axis (lawful, chaotic, neutral). Nine combinations total.

---

### ALLY

A creature is your ally if it is in your adventuring party, your friend, on your side in combat, or designated as such by the rules or DM.

---

### AREA OF EFFECT

An area of effect has a point of origin from which energy erupts. Straight lines from the origin to a location must not be blocked by Total Cover; if blocked, that location is excluded.

If the origin is unseen and an obstruction sits between the creator and the origin, the origin manifests on the near side of the obstruction.

**Shapes:** Cone, Cube, Cylinder, Emanation, Line, Sphere.

---

### ARMOR CLASS

The target number for an attack roll, representing how hard a creature is to hit. Base AC = 10 + Dex modifier. Other base calculations may apply (you choose one; you can't combine them).

---

### ARMOR TRAINING

Allows use of armor without penalty. Without training in Light, Medium, or Heavy armor: Disadvantage on Strength/Dexterity D20 Tests, and you cannot cast spells. Without Shield training: no AC bonus from the Shield.

---

### ATTACK
**Tag:** Action

Make one attack roll with a weapon or Unarmed Strike.

**Equipping/Unequipping:** Equip or unequip one weapon as part of this action (before or after the attack). Includes drawing, picking up, sheathing, stowing, or dropping.
**Moving Between Attacks:** With Extra Attack or similar, you can split your movement between attacks.

---

### ATTACK ROLL

A D20 Test representing an attack with a weapon, Unarmed Strike, or spell.

---

### ATTITUDE

A monster's starting disposition toward a player character: **Friendly**, **Hostile**, or **Indifferent**.

---

### ATTUNEMENT

A magical bond required by some magic items before their properties function. A creature can be Attuned to no more than three magic items at once.

---

### BLINDED
**Tag:** Condition

**Can't See:** Auto-fail any ability check requiring sight.
**Attacks Affected:** Attacks against you have Advantage; your attacks have Disadvantage.

---

### BLINDSIGHT

See within a specified range without physical sight. Within that range, you can see anything not behind Total Cover even while Blinded or in Darkness, including Invisible creatures.

---

### BLOODIED

A creature is Bloodied while at half its Hit Points or fewer.

---

### BONUS ACTION

A special action taken on the same turn as your action. Maximum one per turn; only available if a rule grants one.

---

### BREAKING OBJECTS

**Armor Class (suggested by substance):**
| AC | Substance |
|----|-----------|
| 11 | Cloth, paper, rope |
| 13 | Crystal, glass, ice |
| 15 | Wood |
| 17 | Stone |
| 19 | Iron, steel |
| 21 | Mithral |
| 23 | Adamantine |

**Hit Points (Large or smaller):**
| Size | Fragile | Resilient |
|------|---------|-----------|
| Tiny (bottle, lock) | 2 (1d4) | 5 (2d4) |
| Small (chest, lute) | 3 (1d6) | 10 (3d6) |
| Medium (barrel, chandelier) | 4 (1d8) | 18 (4d8) |
| Large (cart, dining table) | 5 (1d10) | 27 (5d10) |

Huge/Gargantuan objects are divided into Large or smaller sections, each tracked separately.

**Damage Types and Objects:** Objects have Immunity to Poison and Psychic damage. The DM may decide other types are more or less effective. Some materials have Vulnerability (e.g., paper to Fire).
**Damage Threshold:** Big objects (e.g., castle walls) may have a damage threshold (see entry).
**No Ability Scores:** Objects lack ability scores unless assigned; they fail all saves and can't make ability checks.

---

### BRIGHT LIGHT

Normal illumination.

---

### BURNING
**Tag:** Hazard

A burning creature or object takes 1d4 Fire damage at the start of each of its turns. Extinguish by going Prone and rolling on the ground (action), being doused, submerged, or suffocated.

---

### BURROW SPEED

Movement through sand, earth, mud, or ice. Cannot burrow through solid rock without a specific trait.

---

### CAMPAIGN

A series of adventures.

---

### CANTRIP

A level 0 spell, cast without a spell slot.

---

### CARRYING CAPACITY

| Size | Carry | Drag/Lift/Push |
|------|-------|----------------|
| Tiny | Str. × 7.5 lb. | Str. × 15 lb. |
| Small/Medium | Str. × 15 lb. | Str. × 30 lb. |
| Large | Str. × 30 lb. | Str. × 60 lb. |
| Huge | Str. × 60 lb. | Str. × 120 lb. |
| Gargantuan | Str. × 120 lb. | Str. × 240 lb. |

While dragging, lifting, or pushing weight beyond your carry maximum, Speed cannot exceed 5 feet.

---

### CHALLENGE RATING

A summary of a monster's threat level relative to a party of four player characters. Higher CR = more dangerous; lower CR = less so. The DMG provides guidance.

---

### CHARACTER SHEET

A paper or digital record tracking your character's information.

---

### CHARMED
**Tag:** Condition

**Can't Harm the Charmer:** Cannot attack the charmer or target them with damaging abilities or magical effects.
**Social Advantage:** The charmer has Advantage on social ability checks against you.

---

### CLIMBING

Each foot of climbing costs 1 extra foot of movement (2 extra in Difficult Terrain). Ignored if you have a Climb Speed and use it.

A slippery surface or one with few handholds may require a successful DC 15 Strength (Athletics) check (DM's option).

---

### CLIMB SPEED

Used in place of Speed to traverse vertical surfaces without the extra movement cost of climbing.

---

### CONCENTRATION

Some spells and effects require Concentration. If lost, the effect ends. The following break Concentration:

**Another Concentration Effect:** Starting another spell or effect requiring Concentration.
**Damage:** On taking damage, make a Constitution save. DC = 10 or half the damage taken (round down), whichever is higher, max DC 30.
**Incapacitated or Dead:** Concentration ends.

The creator may end Concentration voluntarily at any time (no action).

---

### CONDITION

A temporary game state. Conditions don't stack with themselves (Exhaustion is the exception).

**Conditions:** Blinded, Charmed, Deafened, Exhaustion, Frightened, Grappled, Incapacitated, Invisible, Paralyzed, Petrified, Poisoned, Prone, Restrained, Stunned, Unconscious.

---

### CONE
**Tag:** Area of Effect

Extends in straight lines from a point of origin in a chosen direction. Width at any point along its length equals the distance from the origin (e.g., 15 ft. wide at 15 ft. out). Origin not included in the area unless the creator decides otherwise.

---

### COVER

| Cover | Benefit |
|-------|---------|
| Half Cover | +2 to AC and Dexterity saves |
| Three-Quarters Cover | +5 to AC and Dexterity saves |
| Total Cover | Cannot be targeted directly |

If behind multiple degrees, only the most protective applies.

---

### CRAWLING

Each foot of crawling costs 1 extra foot of movement (2 extra in Difficult Terrain).

---

### CREATURE

Any being in the game, including a player character.

---

### CREATURE TYPE

Every creature has a type tag. Types: Aberration, Beast, Celestial, Construct, Dragon, Elemental, Fey, Fiend, Giant, Humanoid, Monstrosity, Ooze, Plant, Undead.

Types have no rules of their own, but other rules may treat them differently.

---

### CRITICAL HIT

A natural 20 on an attack roll. The attack hits regardless of modifiers or AC. Roll all attack damage dice twice and add them, then add modifiers.

---

### CUBE
**Tag:** Area of Effect

Extends in straight lines from a point of origin on any face of the Cube. Effect specifies the side length. Origin not included unless the creator decides otherwise.

---

### CURSES

Effects that curse a creature or object are removed by *Remove Curse*, *Greater Restoration*, or other magic that explicitly ends curses.

---

### CYLINDER
**Tag:** Area of Effect

Extends in straight lines from a point of origin at the center of the circular top or bottom. Effect specifies radius and height. Origin is included.

---

### D20 TEST

Encompasses ability checks, attack rolls, and saving throws. Anything affecting D20 Tests affects all three.

---

### DAMAGE

Harm that causes a creature or object to lose Hit Points.

---

### DAMAGE ROLL

A die roll, adjusted by modifiers, dealing damage to a target.

---

### DAMAGE THRESHOLD

A creature or object with a damage threshold has Immunity to all damage unless a single attack or effect equals or exceeds the threshold, in which case full damage applies. Damage below the threshold is superficial.

---

### DAMAGE TYPES

| Type | Examples |
|------|----------|
| Acid | Corrosive liquids, digestive enzymes |
| Bludgeoning | Blunt objects, constriction, falling |
| Cold | Freezing water, icy blasts |
| Fire | Flames, unbearable heat |
| Force | Pure magical energy |
| Lightning | Electricity |
| Necrotic | Life-draining energy |
| Piercing | Fangs, puncturing objects |
| Poison | Toxic gas, venom |
| Psychic | Mind-rending energy |
| Radiant | Holy energy, searing radiation |
| Slashing | Claws, cutting objects |
| Thunder | Concussive sound |

---

### DARKNESS

Heavily Obscured area.

---

### DARKVISION

In Dim Light within range, see as if in Bright Light. In Darkness within range, see as if in Dim Light (colors appear as shades of gray in Darkness).

---

### DASH
**Tag:** Action

Gain extra movement equal to your Speed for the current turn (after applying modifiers). With a special speed (Fly, Swim, etc.), you may use that speed instead.

---

### DEAD

A dead creature has 0 HP and cannot regain them without revival magic (e.g., *Raise Dead*, *Revivify*). The spirit knows who is casting and may refuse.

If revived, the creature returns with any ongoing conditions, contagions, or curses still in effect; with 1 fewer Exhaustion level if it had any; and with no Attunements.

---

### DEAFENED
**Tag:** Condition

**Can't Hear:** Auto-fail any ability check requiring hearing.

---

### DEATH SAVING THROW

A player character makes a Death Save when starting their turn at 0 HP.

---

### DEHYDRATION
**Tag:** Hazard

A creature drinking less than half its daily water gains 1 Exhaustion level at day's end. Removed only by drinking the full daily amount.

| Size | Water |
|------|-------|
| Tiny | 1/4 gallon |
| Small | 1 gallon |
| Medium | 1 gallon |
| Large | 4 gallons |
| Huge | 16 gallons |
| Gargantuan | 64 gallons |

---

### DIFFICULT TERRAIN

Each foot of movement costs 1 extra foot. Not cumulative — a space either is or isn't Difficult Terrain.

A space counts as Difficult Terrain if it contains any of: a non-Tiny non-ally creature; furniture sized for creatures your size or larger; heavy snow, ice, rubble, or undergrowth; liquid shin- to waist-deep; an opening sized for a creature one size smaller than you; a slope of 20° or more.

---

### DIFFICULTY CLASS

The target number for an ability check or saving throw.

---

### DIM LIGHT

Lightly Obscured area.

---

### DISADVANTAGE

Roll two d20s and use the lower roll. A roll cannot have more than one Disadvantage; Advantage and Disadvantage on the same roll cancel out.

---

### DISENGAGE
**Tag:** Action

Movement doesn't provoke Opportunity Attacks for the rest of the current turn.

---

### DODGE
**Tag:** Action

Until the start of your next turn: attacks against you have Disadvantage (if you can see the attacker), and you make Dexterity saves with Advantage. Lost if Incapacitated or Speed 0.

---

### EMANATION
**Tag:** Area of Effect

Extends in straight lines from a creature or object in all directions. Moves with its origin (unless instantaneous or stationary). Origin not included unless the creator decides otherwise.

---

### ENCOUNTER

A scene that is part of one or more of the three pillars: social interaction, exploration, or combat.

---

### ENEMY

A creature fighting against you, working to harm you, or designated as such by the rules or DM.

---

### EXHAUSTION
**Tag:** Condition

**Cumulative:** Each application adds 1 Exhaustion level. Death at level 6.
**D20 Tests Affected:** Reduce all D20 Tests by 2 × Exhaustion level.
**Speed Reduced:** Reduce Speed by 5 ft. × Exhaustion level.
**Removing Levels:** A Long Rest removes 1 level. Condition ends at level 0.

---

### EXPERIENCE POINTS

Awarded by the DM for overcoming challenges and completing adventures. Crossing thresholds raises a character's level.

---

### EXPERTISE

Doubles your Proficiency Bonus on ability checks made with a chosen skill proficiency. Cannot stack on the same skill (no doubling twice).

---

### FALLING
**Tag:** Hazard

1d6 Bludgeoning damage per 10 feet fallen, max 20d6. The creature lands Prone unless it takes no damage.

Falling into liquid: Reaction to make a DC 15 Strength (Athletics) or Dexterity (Acrobatics) check; success halves any fall damage.

---

### FLYING

While flying, you fall if Incapacitated, Prone, or your Fly Speed is reduced to 0 — unless you can hover.

---

### FLY SPEED

Movement through the air. You can stay aloft until you land, fall, or die.

---

### FRIENDLY
**Tag:** Attitude

Views you favorably. Advantage on ability checks to influence a Friendly creature.

---

### FRIGHTENED
**Tag:** Condition

**Ability Checks and Attacks Affected:** Disadvantage while the source of fear is in line of sight.
**Can't Approach:** Cannot willingly move closer to the source of fear.

---

### GRAPPLED
**Tag:** Condition

**Speed 0:** Cannot increase.
**Attacks Affected:** Disadvantage on attacks against any target other than the grappler.
**Movable:** The grappler can drag or carry you, but each foot costs 1 extra foot unless you're Tiny or two or more sizes smaller.

---

### GRAPPLING

Typically initiated by an Unarmed Strike. Some monsters have special grapple attacks.

**Grappled Condition:** Successfully grappling imposes the Grappled condition.
**One Grapple per Hand:** Requires a free hand (or designated body part). One creature per part.
**Escaping a Grapple:** Action — Strength (Athletics) or Dexterity (Acrobatics) check vs. the escape DC. Condition also ends if the grappler is Incapacitated or the target moves outside grapple range.

---

### HAZARD

An environmental danger. Includes Burning, Dehydration, Falling, Malnutrition, Suffocation.

---

### HEALING

How a creature regains Hit Points.

---

### HEAVILY OBSCURED

You have the Blinded condition for purposes of seeing in or into a Heavily Obscured space.

---

### HELP
**Tag:** Action

Choose one:
**Assist an Ability Check:** Pick a skill or tool proficiency you have and one nearby ally. They gain Advantage on their next ability check with that skill or tool. Expires at start of your next turn.
**Assist an Attack Roll:** Distract an enemy within 5 ft. — one ally gains Advantage on their next attack against that enemy. Expires at start of your next turn.

---

### HEROIC INSPIRATION

A player character can expend Heroic Inspiration to reroll any die immediately after rolling; must use the new roll. If gained while you already have it, it's lost unless you give it to a player character who lacks it.

---

### HIDE
**Tag:** Action

Make a DC 15 Dexterity (Stealth) check while Heavily Obscured or behind Three-Quarters/Total Cover, and out of any enemy's line of sight. On success, gain the Invisible condition. Note your check total — that's the DC for a Wisdom (Perception) check to find you.

The condition ends when: you make a sound louder than a whisper, an enemy finds you, you make an attack roll, or you cast a spell with a Verbal component.

---

### HIGH JUMP

Leap up 3 + Strength modifier feet (min 0) after moving at least 10 ft. on foot. Standing High Jump = half that. Each foot jumped costs a foot of movement.

You can extend your arms half your height above yourself; total reach = jump height + 1½ × your height.

---

### HIT POINT DICE

Help determine a player character's HP maximum. Most monsters also have Hit Dice. A creature can spend Hit Dice during a Short Rest to regain HP.

---

### HIT POINTS

A measure of how hard it is to kill a creature or destroy an object. Cannot exceed your maximum or fall below 0.

---

### HOSTILE
**Tag:** Attitude

Views you unfavorably. Disadvantage on ability checks to influence a Hostile creature.

---

### HOVER

Some creatures or effects allow hovering. Hovering while flying prevents falling in certain circumstances.

---

### ILLUSIONS

Effects that create magical illusions. The effect specifies what's deceived (which senses or mental faculties).

A manifest illusion is insubstantial and weightless but appears affected by the environment as if real (casts shadows, etc.) unless specified otherwise.

---

### IMMUNITY

Damage type or condition has no effect on you.

---

### IMPROVISED WEAPONS

A makeshift weapon (broken glass, table leg, frying pan). A Simple/Martial weapon used contrary to its design also counts (e.g., melee with a Ranged weapon, or throwing a Melee weapon without the Thrown property).

**Proficiency:** No Proficiency Bonus added to attack rolls.
**Damage:** 1d4 of a type chosen by the DM.
**Range:** Thrown — 20/60 ft.
**Weapon Equivalents:** If it resembles a real weapon, the DM may treat it as that weapon (e.g., table leg = Club).

---

### INCAPACITATED
**Tag:** Condition

**Inactive:** No actions, Bonus Actions, or Reactions.
**No Concentration:** Concentration ends.
**Speechless:** Cannot speak.
**Surprised:** If Incapacitated when rolling Initiative, Disadvantage on the roll.

---

### INDIFFERENT
**Tag:** Attitude

The default attitude. No desire to help or hinder you.

---

### INFLUENCE
**Tag:** Action

Urge a monster to do something. The DM determines whether the monster is willing, unwilling, or hesitant:

**Willing:** No check needed; the monster complies as it prefers.
**Unwilling:** No check; it refuses.
**Hesitant:** Make an ability check, modified by attitude (Indifferent/Friendly/Hostile). DC = 15 or the monster's Intelligence score, whichever is higher. On failure, you must wait 24 hours (or DM-set duration) before urging the same way again.

| Ability Check | Interaction |
|---------------|-------------|
| Charisma (Deception) | Deceiving a monster that understands you |
| Charisma (Intimidation) | Intimidating a monster |
| Charisma (Performance) | Amusing a monster |
| Charisma (Persuasion) | Persuading a monster that understands you |
| Wisdom (Animal Handling) | Gently coaxing a Beast or Monstrosity |

---

### INITIATIVE

Determines turn order in combat. Optionally use Initiative scores (10 + Dex modifier) instead of rolling. Advantage on Initiative rolls = +5 to score; Disadvantage = -5.

---

### INVISIBLE
**Tag:** Condition

**Surprise:** Advantage on Initiative rolls.
**Concealed:** Unaffected by effects requiring you to be seen, unless the creator can somehow see you. Worn/carried gear is also concealed.
**Attacks Affected:** Attacks against you have Disadvantage; your attacks have Advantage. (No benefit against a creature that can somehow see you.)

---

### JUMPING

Either a Long Jump (horizontal) or High Jump (vertical).

---

### KNOCKING OUT A CREATURE

When reducing a creature to 0 HP with a melee attack, you may instead reduce it to 1 HP. The creature is Unconscious and starts a Short Rest. Remains Unconscious until it regains any HP or someone administers first aid (DC 10 Wisdom [Medicine] check, action).

---

### LIGHTLY OBSCURED

Disadvantage on Wisdom (Perception) checks to see something in this space.

---

### LINE
**Tag:** Area of Effect

Extends in a straight path from origin along its length, with a defined width. Origin not included unless the creator decides otherwise.

---

### LONG JUMP

Leap up to your Strength score in feet horizontally after moving at least 10 ft. Standing Long Jump = half that. Each foot jumped costs a foot of movement.

Landing in Difficult Terrain: DC 10 Dexterity (Acrobatics) check or fall Prone. To clear a low obstacle (≤ a quarter of jump distance), DC 10 Strength (Athletics) check at the DM's option.

---

### LONG REST

At least 8 hours of downtime: 6+ hours of sleep and no more than 2 hours of light activity (reading, talking, eating, standing watch). You have the Unconscious condition while sleeping. Must wait 16 hours before starting another.

**Benefits (must start with at least 1 HP):**
- Regain all HP and all spent Hit Point Dice; HP maximum returns to normal if reduced.
- Reduced ability scores return to normal.
- Exhaustion level decreases by 1.
- Some features recharge as specified.

**Interruptions:** Rolling Initiative, casting a non-cantrip spell, taking damage, or 1 hour of walking/exertion. If interrupted after 1+ hour of rest, you gain Short Rest benefits. You can resume immediately, but the rest requires +1 hour per interruption.

---

### MAGIC
**Tag:** Action

Cast a spell with a casting time of an action, or use a feature/magic item requiring a Magic action. Spells with casting times of 1 minute or longer require taking the Magic action each turn while maintaining Concentration; if Concentration breaks, the spell fails (no slot expended).

---

### MAGICAL EFFECT

An effect created by a spell, magic item, or phenomenon labeled magical by a rule.

---

### MALNUTRITION
**Tag:** Hazard

A creature eating less than half its daily food must succeed on a DC 10 Constitution save or gain 1 Exhaustion level at day's end. Eating nothing for 5 days adds 1 level on day 5 and 1 each day thereafter. Removed only by eating a full daily amount.

| Size | Food |
|------|------|
| Tiny | 1/4 pound |
| Small | 1 pound |
| Medium | 1 pound |
| Large | 4 pounds |
| Huge | 16 pounds |
| Gargantuan | 64 pounds |

---

### MONSTER

A creature controlled by the DM, even a benevolent one.

---

### NONPLAYER CHARACTER

A monster with a personal name and distinct personality.

---

### OBJECT

A nonliving, distinct thing. Composite things (buildings) consist of multiple objects.

---

### OCCUPIED SPACE

A space containing a creature or completely filled by objects.

---

### OPPORTUNITY ATTACKS

When a creature you can see leaves your reach using its action, Bonus Action, Reaction, or one of its speeds, you can take a Reaction to make one melee attack with a weapon or Unarmed Strike. The attack occurs just before the creature leaves your reach.

---

### PARALYZED
**Tag:** Condition

**Incapacitated:** Has the Incapacitated condition.
**Speed 0:** Cannot increase.
**Saving Throws Affected:** Auto-fail Strength and Dexterity saves.
**Attacks Affected:** Attacks against you have Advantage.
**Automatic Critical Hits:** Any hit from within 5 ft. is a Critical Hit.

---

### PASSIVE PERCEPTION

Reflects general awareness. Score = 10 + Wisdom (Perception) bonus. Advantage on such checks: +5 to score. Disadvantage: -5.

---

### PER DAY

A "per day" feature requires a Long Rest to recharge once exhausted.

---

### PETRIFIED
**Tag:** Condition

**Turned to Inanimate Substance:** Transformed into solid substance (usually stone), along with worn/carried nonmagical objects. Weight ×10. Aging ceases.
**Incapacitated:** Has the Incapacitated condition.
**Speed 0:** Cannot increase.
**Attacks Affected:** Attacks against you have Advantage.
**Saving Throws Affected:** Auto-fail Strength and Dexterity saves.
**Resist Damage:** Resistance to all damage.
**Poison Immunity:** Immunity to the Poisoned condition.

---

### PLAYER CHARACTER

A character controlled by a player.

---

### POISONED
**Tag:** Condition

**Ability Checks and Attacks Affected:** Disadvantage on attack rolls and ability checks.

---

### POSSESSION

A creature possessed by another. The effect defines how it operates. Prevented by *Protection from Evil and Good*; ended by *Dispel Evil and Good*.

---

### PROFICIENCY

Add your Proficiency Bonus to D20 Tests using something you're proficient with — a skill, save, weapon, or tool.

---

### PRONE
**Tag:** Condition

**Restricted Movement:** Either crawl, or spend half your Speed (round down) to right yourself and end the condition. Cannot stand if Speed 0.
**Attacks Affected:** Disadvantage on your attacks. Attacks from within 5 ft. against you have Advantage; attacks from beyond 5 ft. against you have Disadvantage.

---

### REACTION

A special action triggered by a defined event. Available on any creature's turn. After taking one, you cannot take another until the start of your next turn. Opportunity Attack is the universal Reaction.

---

### READY
**Tag:** Action

Wait for a triggering circumstance, then take a Reaction before the start of your next turn. Define the trigger and the action (or movement up to your Speed).

When readying a spell, cast it as normal (expending resources), but hold it via Concentration until the trigger or the start of your next turn. If Concentration breaks, the spell dissipates without effect. Ready-able spells must have a casting time of an action.

---

### RESISTANCE

Damage of that type is halved against you (round down). Resistance applies only once per damage instance.

---

### RESTRAINED
**Tag:** Condition

**Speed 0:** Cannot increase.
**Attacks Affected:** Attacks against you have Advantage; your attacks have Disadvantage.
**Saving Throws Affected:** Disadvantage on Dexterity saves.

---

### RITUAL

A spell with the Ritual tag, prepared, can be cast as a Ritual: takes 10 minutes longer, expends no slot. Cannot be upcast as a Ritual.

---

### ROUND DOWN

When dividing or multiplying produces a fraction, round down — even at 0.5 or higher — unless a rule explicitly says otherwise.

---

### SAVE

Another name for a saving throw.

---

### SAVING THROW

A D20 Test to avoid or resist a threat. Made only when a rule requires it. You may choose to fail without rolling. A target lacking the relevant ability score auto-fails.

---

### SEARCH
**Tag:** Action

Make a Wisdom check to discern something not obvious.

| Skill | Thing to Detect |
|-------|----------------|
| Insight | Creature's state of mind |
| Medicine | Creature's ailment or cause of death |
| Perception | Concealed creature or object |
| Survival | Tracks or food |

---

### SHAPE-SHIFTING

Effects like *Wild Shape* or *Polymorph* let you change form. The effect specifies what happens. Unless stated otherwise, ongoing effects (conditions, spells, curses) carry over between forms. You revert to true form on death.

---

### SHORT REST

A 1-hour period of light activity (reading, talking, eating, watching). Must start with 1+ HP.

**Benefits:**
- **Spend Hit Point Dice:** Spend any number; for each, roll the die + Constitution modifier and regain that much HP (min 1). Decide one die at a time.
- Some features recharge as specified.

**Interruptions:** Rolling Initiative, casting a non-cantrip spell, or taking damage. An interrupted Short Rest grants no benefits.

---

### SIMULTANEOUS EFFECTS

If two or more things happen at the same time on a turn, the person whose turn it is decides the order.

---

### SIZE

Tiny, Small, Medium, Large, Huge, or Gargantuan. Determines combat space and (for objects) HP.

---

### SKILL

An area of specialization tied to an ability check. Proficiency lets you add your Proficiency Bonus to associated checks.

---

### SPEED

Distance in feet you can move on your turn.

**Special Speeds:** Burrow, Climb, Fly, or Swim Speed. With multiple speeds, choose which to use; you can switch mid-move. When switching, subtract distance already moved from the new speed; if 0 or less, you can't use it that turn.

**Changes to Your Speeds:** If your Speed changes, special speeds change by the same amount or proportion. (Speed reduced to 0 → all special speeds reduced to 0. Speed halved → all halved.)

---

### SPELL

A magical effect described by chapter 7 of the rules.

---

### SPELL ATTACK

An attack roll made as part of a spell or magical effect.

---

### SPELLCASTING FOCUS

An object that can substitute for a spell's Material components, provided the materials aren't consumed and lack a specified cost. Class features specify which Focus types are usable.

---

### SPHERE
**Tag:** Area of Effect

Extends in straight lines from a point of origin in all directions. Effect specifies the radius. Origin is included.

---

### STABLE

A creature is Stable if it has 0 HP but isn't required to make Death Saves.

---

### STAT BLOCK

A monster's game statistics, listed after its name:

**Size, Creature Type, Alignment, AC, Initiative, HP, Speed, Ability Scores, Skills, Resistances and Vulnerabilities, Immunities, Gear, Senses, Languages, CR, Traits, Actions, Bonus Actions, Reactions.**

**Attack Notation:** Identifies melee/ranged, attack roll bonus, reach/range, and on-hit effects. Single target unless stated otherwise.
**Saving Throw Effect Notation:** Identifies the save type, DC, affected creatures, and outcomes on success/failure.
**Damage Notation:** Provides both a static number and a die expression (e.g., "4 (1d4 + 2)"). The DM picks which to use; never both.

---

### STUDY
**Tag:** Action

Make an Intelligence check to recall a piece of knowledge.

| Skill | Areas |
|-------|-------|
| Arcana | Spells, magic items, eldritch symbols, magical traditions, planes of existence; Aberrations, Constructs, Elementals, Fey, Monstrosities |
| History | Historic events and people, ancient civilizations, wars; Giants and Humanoids |
| Investigation | Traps, ciphers, riddles, gadgetry |
| Nature | Terrain, flora, weather; Beasts, Dragons, Oozes, Plants |
| Religion | Deities, religious hierarchies and rites, holy symbols, cults; Celestials, Fiends, Undead |

---

### STUNNED
**Tag:** Condition

**Incapacitated:** Has the Incapacitated condition.
**Saving Throws Affected:** Auto-fail Strength and Dexterity saves.
**Attacks Affected:** Attacks against you have Advantage.

---

### SUFFOCATION
**Tag:** Hazard

Hold breath for 1 + Constitution modifier minutes (min 30 seconds). When out of breath or choking, gain 1 Exhaustion level at end of each turn. Once breathing resumes, all suffocation-caused Exhaustion is removed.

---

### SURPRISE

A creature caught unawares at the start of combat is surprised and has Disadvantage on its Initiative roll.

---

### SWIMMING

Each foot of swimming costs 1 extra foot (2 extra in Difficult Terrain). Ignored if you have a Swim Speed and use it. Rough water may require a successful DC 15 Strength (Athletics) check (DM's option).

---

### SWIM SPEED

Swim without the extra movement cost.

---

### TARGET

The creature or object targeted by an attack roll, forced into a save by an effect, or selected to receive a spell or other phenomenon.

---

### TELEPATHY

Magical mental communication within a specified range. Unless stated otherwise, no shared language is needed for the contacted creature to understand — but the contacted creature must understand at least one language or be telepathic itself.

The telepath need not see the contact. Either party may end contact at any time (no action). Contact is broken by Incapacitation of either party, exceeding range, or the telepath contacting a different creature within range.

A non-telepath can receive but not initiate telepathic contact. Once started, the non-telepath can communicate mentally with the telepath until the connection ends.

---

### TELEPORTATION

A magical transport that disappears and reappears you elsewhere instantly without crossing intervening space. No movement is expended (unless stated). Never provokes Opportunity Attacks.

Worn/carried gear teleports with you. A creature you're touching doesn't teleport with you unless the effect says so. If the destination is occupied or blocked, you appear in the nearest unoccupied space of your choice. The effect specifies whether you must see the destination.

---

### TEMPORARY HIT POINTS

Granted by certain effects, acting as a buffer against losing real HP.

---

### TREMORSENSE

Pinpoints location of creatures and moving objects within range, provided both you and the target are in contact with the same surface (ground, wall, ceiling) or liquid. Cannot detect things in the air; not a form of sight.

---

### TRUESIGHT

Within the specified range, vision pierces:
- **Darkness:** See through normal and magical Darkness.
- **Invisibility:** See Invisible creatures and objects.
- **Visual Illusions:** Visual illusions appear transparent; auto-succeed on saves against them.
- **Transformations:** Discern the true form of creatures or objects transformed by magic.
- **Ethereal Plane:** See into the Ethereal Plane.

---

### UNARMED STRIKE

A melee attack using your body — punch, kick, head-butt, or similar — within 5 ft. Choose one effect:

**Damage:** Attack roll (Strength modifier + Proficiency Bonus). On hit: 1 + Strength modifier Bludgeoning damage.
**Grapple:** Target makes a Strength or Dexterity save (target's choice) vs. DC 8 + Strength modifier + Proficiency Bonus. On failure, target is Grappled. Requires the target be no more than one size larger and a free hand.
**Shove:** Target makes a Strength or Dexterity save (target's choice) vs. DC 8 + Strength modifier + Proficiency Bonus. On failure, push 5 ft. away or knock Prone (your choice). Target must be no more than one size larger.

---

### UNCONSCIOUS
**Tag:** Condition

**Inert:** Has the Incapacitated and Prone conditions; drops what it's holding. Remains Prone after the condition ends.
**Speed 0:** Cannot increase.
**Attacks Affected:** Attacks against you have Advantage.
**Saving Throws Affected:** Auto-fail Strength and Dexterity saves.
**Automatic Critical Hits:** Any hit from within 5 ft. is a Critical Hit.
**Unaware:** Unaware of your surroundings.

---

### UNOCCUPIED SPACE

A space with no creatures and not completely filled by objects.

---

### UTILIZE
**Tag:** Action

Used when an object requires an action to operate (rather than the typical incidental interaction).

---

### VULNERABILITY

Damage of that type is doubled against you. Vulnerability applies only once per damage instance.

---

### WEAPON

An object in the Simple or Martial weapon category.

---

### WEAPON ATTACK

An attack roll made with a weapon.
