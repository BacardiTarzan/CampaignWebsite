# Languages

Every character knows at least three languages: **Common** plus two additional languages chosen or rolled from the Standard Languages table. Your class or other features may grant additional languages.

Knowledge of a language means your character can communicate in it, read it, and write it.

---

## Standard Languages

These languages are widespread across the setting. Every player character knows Common. The other standard languages originated with prominent species and have since spread widely.

| d12 | Language | Typical Speakers |
|-----|----------|-----------------|
| — | Common | Humans and most species |
| 1 | Common Sign Language | Deaf communities |
| 2 | Draconic | Dragons, Dragonborn |
| 3–4 | Dwarvish | Dwarves |
| 5–6 | Elvish | Elves |
| 7 | Giant | Giants, Goliaths |
| 8 | Gnomish | Gnomes |
| 9 | Goblin | Goblinoids |
| 10–11 | Halfling | Halflings |
| 12 | Orc | Orcs |

---

## Rare Languages

Rare languages are either secret or derived from other planes of existence, and less widespread in the Material Plane. Some class features or feats allow a character to learn a rare language.

| Language | Typical Speakers |
|----------|-----------------|
| Abyssal | Demons, fiends of the Abyss |
| Celestial | Angels, celestials |
| Deep Speech | Aberrations (mind flayers, beholders) |
| Druidic | Druids only (secret language) |
| Infernal | Devils, fiends of the Nine Hells |
| Primordial* | Elementals |
| Sylvan | Fey creatures |
| Thieves' Cant | Rogues, criminal organizations (secret) |
| Undercommon | Underdark traders and inhabitants |

*Primordial includes four dialects: **Aquan** (water), **Auran** (air), **Ignan** (fire), and **Terran** (earth). Creatures that know one dialect can communicate with those who know a different one.

---

## Rules Notes

- **Druidic** and **Thieves' Cant** are secret languages — non-members aren't taught them.
- Characters can learn additional languages through the **Linguist** feat (if used) or DM-granted story rewards.
- Some species automatically grant specific languages beyond Common — check each species description.
