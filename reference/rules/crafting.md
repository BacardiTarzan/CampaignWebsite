# Crafting (Rules)

### Crafting Nonmagical Items

To craft a nonmagical item, you need the required tool and proficiency with it, raw materials worth half the item's purchase cost (round down), and time (divide purchase cost in GP by 10, rounding fractions up to a full day of 8-hour workdays).

Multiple characters can combine efforts to shorten the crafting time; divide the time by the number of characters working. Normally only one other character can assist.

### Brewing Potions of Healing

A character proficient with the Herbalism Kit can create a Potion of Healing using that kit and 25 GP of raw materials over 1 day (8 hours of work).

### Scribing Spell Scrolls

**Prerequisites.** You must have proficiency in the Arcana skill or with Calligrapher's Tools, and have the spell prepared on each day of the inscription. Any Material components required by the spell must be on hand; consumed components are only consumed when you complete the scroll. The scroll's spell uses your spell save DC and spell attack bonus.

**Cantrips.** The version on the scroll works as if the caster were your level.
