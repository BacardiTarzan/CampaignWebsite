# Magic Items (Rules)

### Identifying a Magic Item

Handling a magic item gives you a sense it is extraordinary, but learning its properties requires one of the following:

- Cast the **Identify** spell on it.
- Focus on the item during a **Short Rest** while in physical contact with it. At the end of the rest, you learn its properties and how to use them (but not any curse the item might bear).

### Attunement

Some magic items require Attunement before you can use their magical properties. Without Attunement, you gain only the item's nonmagical benefits.

**Attuning.** Spend a Short Rest focused on only that item while in physical contact with it (can't be the same Short Rest used to identify it). If the Short Rest is interrupted, the attempt fails.

**Limit.** You can be attuned to no more than three magic items at a time, and no more than one copy of the same item.

**Ending Attunement.** Attunement ends if you no longer satisfy the prerequisites for it, if the item has been more than 100 feet away for at least 24 hours, if you die, or if another creature attunes to the item. You can also voluntarily end Attunement by spending another Short Rest focused on the item, unless the item is cursed.

### Wearing and Wielding

A magic item meant to be worn must be donned in the intended fashion. In most cases, a magic item that's meant to be worn can fit a creature regardless of size or build.

You can't normally wear more than one of each of the following: pair of footwear, pair of gloves or gauntlets, pair of bracers, suit of armor, item of headwear, or cloak. Items that come in pairs (boots, bracers, gauntlets, gloves) impart their benefits only if both items of the pair are worn.
