# AASIMAR
**Creature Type:** Humanoid
**Size:** Medium (4–7 ft.) or Small (2–4 ft.), chosen at character creation
**Speed:** 30 feet
**Lifespan:** Up to 160 years

### Traits
**Celestial Resistance:** Resistance to Necrotic and Radiant damage.

**Darkvision:** 60 feet.

**Healing Hands:** Magic Action — touch a creature and roll a number of d4s equal to your Proficiency Bonus; restore that many HP. Once per Long Rest.

**Light Bearer:** Know the Light cantrip (Charisma spellcasting).

**Celestial Revelation (Level 3):** Bonus Action — transform for 1 minute (once per Long Rest). While transformed, once per turn deal extra damage equal to your Proficiency Bonus (Necrotic for Necrotic Shroud; Radiant for the other two). Choose one form each time:
- **Heavenly Wings:** Gain Fly Speed = Speed.
- **Inner Radiance:** Shed Bright Light 10 ft. / Dim Light 10 ft.; creatures within 10 ft. take Radiant damage = Proficiency Bonus at end of each of your turns.
- **Necrotic Shroud:** Creatures other than allies within 10 ft. make a Charisma save (DC 8 + Cha modifier + Prof.) or are Frightened until end of your next turn.
