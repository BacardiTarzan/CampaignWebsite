# ORC
**Creature Type:** Humanoid
**Size:** Medium (6–7 ft.)
**Speed:** 30 feet

### Traits
**Adrenaline Rush:** Take the Dash action as a Bonus Action and gain Temp HP equal to your Proficiency Bonus. Uses = Proficiency Bonus; recharge on Short or Long Rest.

**Darkvision:** 120 feet.

**Relentless Endurance:** When reduced to 0 HP but not killed outright, drop to 1 HP instead. Once per Long Rest.
