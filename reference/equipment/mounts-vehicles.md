# Mounts and Vehicles

### Mounts and Other Animals

An animal pulling a vehicle can move weight up to five times its carrying capacity, including the vehicle's weight. If multiple animals pull the same vehicle, add their carrying capacities together.

### Airborne and Waterborne Vehicles

Ships sailing against a strong wind move at half speed. In a dead calm, waterborne ships can't move under sail and must be rowed. Keelboats and Rowboats are used on lakes and rivers; add the speed of the current (typically 3 mph) if going downstream. A Rowboat weighs 100 pounds and can be carried.

Repairing 1 Hit Point of damage requires 1 day and costs 20 GP. In a city shipyard, repair time and cost are halved.
