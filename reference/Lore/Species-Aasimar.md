# Aasimar

Aasimar are vanishingly rare in Aldermark, and they appear at random. They are born to ordinary families with no trace of celestial heritage themselves — a baker and a smith may raise five unremarkable children before their sixth is born with golden eyes and a warmth that lingers on whatever they touch.

In Aldermark's small villages, an aasimar is treated like a living miracle. Folk approach with reverence and a careful distance, unsure whether to bow or speak. The priest invites them to the altar; the local lord offers them shelter in the keep. To a farming community that has never met a wizard, let alone an angel, an aasimar might as well be a god.

The deeper truth is older. In an age now centuries past, beings from the Upper Planes walked Aldermark openly. Aasimar are echoes of that age — descendants of celestial blood that has thinned across generations. That contact has not been common for a very long time.

The fact that aasimar have begun appearing again, however slowly, has not gone unnoticed by the few who keep such records.

## Adventuring Hooks
- A calling you don't yet understand, soft but persistent
- The burden of constant reverence from people who have no idea what you actually are
- A search for what you are, and what may be returning with you
- Escape from a village or temple that wants to keep you as a relic
