# WIZARD

**Primary Ability:** Intelligence
**Hit Point Die:** d6
**Saving Throws:** Intelligence, Wisdom
**Armor Training:** None
**Weapon Proficiencies:** Simple weapons
**Skill Proficiencies (choose 2):** Arcana, History, Insight, Investigation, Medicine, Nature, Religion
**Starting Equipment:** (A) 2 Daggers, Arcane Focus (Quarterstaff), Robe, Spellbook, Scholar's Pack, 5 GP; or (B) 55 GP

### Key Features

**Spellcasting (Level 1):** Intelligence-based. Spellbook starts with 6 level 1 spells; add 2 per level gained. Prepare from your spellbook; change prepared list on each Long Rest. Use Arcane Focus or spellbook as focus.

**Ritual Adept (Level 1):** Cast any Ritual spell from your spellbook without preparing it (must read from the book).

**Arcane Recovery (Level 1):** On Short Rest, recover spell slots totaling up to half Wizard level (round up); no slot above level 5. Once per Long Rest.

**Scholar (Level 2):** Gain Expertise in one of: Arcana, History, Investigation, Medicine, Nature, or Religion.

**Memorize Spell (Level 5):** After a Short Rest, swap one prepared spell for another from your spellbook.

**Spell Mastery (Level 18):** Choose one level 1 and one level 2 spell from your spellbook — always prepared; can cast at lowest level without expending a slot. Change choices on Long Rest.

**Epic Boon (Level 19):** Gain an Epic Boon feat (Boon of Spell Recall recommended).

**Signature Spells (Level 20):** Choose two level 3 spells — always prepared; can each cast once at level 3 without a slot per Short or Long Rest.

### Copying Spells into Your Spellbook

- Each spell level costs **2 hours** and **50 GP** to transcribe.
- Copying from your own book: **1 hour** and **10 GP** per spell level.

### Subclasses

#### Abjurer
- **Abjuration Savant (Level 3):** Add two Abjuration spells (level 0–2) to spellbook for free; add one free Abjuration spell per new spell slot tier.
- **Arcane Ward (Level 3):** When casting an Abjuration spell with a slot, create a ward (max HP = 2× Wizard level + Int modifier). Ward absorbs damage; replenished by casting Abjuration spells. Once per Long Rest to create.
- **Projected Ward (Level 6):** Reaction — have your Arcane Ward absorb damage dealt to a creature within 30 ft.
- **Spell Breaker (Level 10):** Always have Counterspell and Dispel Magic prepared; cast Dispel Magic as Bonus Action + add Prof. Bonus to its check; spell slot not expended if either spell fails.
- **Spell Resistance (Level 14):** Advantage on saves vs. spells; Resistance to spell damage.

#### Diviner
- **Divination Savant (Level 3):** Add two Divination spells (level 0–2) to spellbook for free; add one free Divination spell per new spell slot tier.
- **Portent (Level 3):** Roll two d20s after Long Rest; use either result (before the roll) to replace any D20 Test made by you or a creature you see. Use each once.
- **Expert Divination (Level 6):** When casting a Divination spell with a level 2+ slot, regain one lower-level slot (max level 5).
- **The Third Eye (Level 10):** Bonus Action — choose one benefit until Short/Long Rest: Darkvision 120 ft., read any language, or cast See Invisibility for free.
- **Greater Portent (Level 14):** Roll three d20s for Portent instead of two.

#### Evoker
- **Evocation Savant (Level 3):** Add two Evocation spells (level 0–2) to spellbook for free; add one free Evocation spell per new spell slot tier.
- **Potent Cantrip (Level 3):** On a missed attack or successful save against your cantrip, target takes half damage (no other effects).
- **Sculpt Spells (Level 6):** When casting an Evocation spell, choose up to 1 + spell level creatures — they auto-succeed on saves and take no damage.
- **Empowered Evocation (Level 10):** Add Intelligence modifier to one damage roll of any Wizard Evocation spell.
- **Overchannel (Level 14):** When casting a Wizard spell with slots 1–5, deal maximum damage. First use per Long Rest: no cost. Each subsequent use before Long Rest: take 2d12 Necrotic per spell level (+1d12 per level per extra use). Ignores Resistance/Immunity.

#### Illusionist
- **Illusion Savant (Level 3):** Add two Illusion spells (level 0–2) to spellbook for free; add one free Illusion spell per new spell slot tier.
- **Improved Illusions (Level 3):** Cast Illusion spells without Verbal components; Illusion spells with 10+ ft. range gain +60 ft. Know Minor Illusion (create both sound and image at once; cast as Bonus Action).
- **Phantasmal Creatures (Level 6):** Always have Summon Beast and Summon Fey prepared; can change their school to Illusion; cast each once without a slot per Long Rest (summoned creature has half HP without a slot).
- **Illusory Self (Level 10):** Reaction — an attack that hits you misses instead (illusion absorbs it); once per Short/Long Rest or expend a level 2+ slot.
- **Illusory Reality (Level 14):** When casting an Illusion spell with a slot, use a Bonus Action to make one inanimate nonmagical object part of the illusion real for 1 minute (cannot deal damage or impose conditions).
