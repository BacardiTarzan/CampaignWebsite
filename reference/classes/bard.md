# BARD

**Primary Ability:** Charisma
**Hit Point Die:** d8
**Saving Throws:** Dexterity, Charisma
**Armor Training:** Light armor
**Weapon Proficiencies:** Simple weapons
**Tool Proficiencies:** 3 Musical Instruments of your choice
**Skill Proficiencies:** Choose any 3
**Starting Equipment:** (A) Leather Armor, 2 Daggers, Musical Instrument, Entertainer's Pack, 19 GP; or (B) 90 GP

### Key Features

**Bardic Inspiration (Level 1):** Bonus Action — grant one creature within 60 ft. a Bardic Inspiration die (d6, scales to d12). Used to add to a failed D20 Test within 1 hour. Uses = Charisma modifier (min 1); recharge on Long Rest.

**Spellcasting (Level 1):** Charisma-based. Use a Musical Instrument as a focus.

**Expertise (Level 2):** Gain Expertise in 2 skill proficiencies. Gain 2 more at level 9.

**Jack of All Trades (Level 2):** Add half Proficiency Bonus to ability checks using skills you lack proficiency in.

**Font of Inspiration (Level 5):** Regain all Bardic Inspiration uses on Short or Long Rest. Can expend a spell slot to regain one use.

**Countercharm (Level 7):** Reaction — when you or a nearby creature fails a save vs. Charmed or Frightened, reroll the save with Advantage.

**Magical Secrets (Level 10):** When your Prepared Spells number increases, new spells can come from the Bard, Cleric, Druid, or Wizard lists.

**Superior Inspiration (Level 18):** On rolling Initiative, regain Bardic Inspiration uses until you have at least 2.

**Epic Boon (Level 19):** Gain an Epic Boon feat (Boon of Spell Recall recommended).

**Words of Creation (Level 20):** Always have Power Word Heal and Power Word Kill prepared; can target a second creature within 10 ft. of the first when casting either.

### Subclasses

#### College of Dance
- **Dazzling Footwork (Level 3):** Unarmored Defense (AC = 10 + Dex + Cha), Advantage on dance Performance checks, make Unarmed Strike when expending Bardic Inspiration, deal Bardic die + Dex modifier Bludgeoning damage with unarmed strikes.
- **Inspiring Movement (Level 6):** Reaction + Bardic Inspiration — move up to half Speed; one ally within 30 ft. can also move.
- **Tandem Footwork (Level 6):** On rolling Initiative, expend Bardic Inspiration to grant allies within 30 ft. an Initiative bonus equal to the roll.
- **Leading Evasion (Level 14):** Evasion on Dexterity saves; can share the benefit with creatures within 5 ft.

#### College of Glamour
- **Beguiling Magic (Level 3):** Always have Charm Person and Mirror Image prepared. After casting an Enchantment or Illusion spell, can Charm or Frighten one creature (Wisdom save). Resets on Long Rest or by expending Bardic Inspiration.
- **Mantle of Inspiration (Level 3):** Bonus Action + Bardic Inspiration — grant Temp HP = 2× die roll to a number of creatures equal to Cha modifier; each can move as a Reaction.
- **Mantle of Majesty (Level 6):** Always have Command prepared; cast it as a Bonus Action without a spell slot for 1 minute; Charmed creatures auto-fail their save.
- **Unbreakable Majesty (Level 14):** Bonus Action — attackers must succeed on a Charisma save or their first hit against you per turn misses instead.

#### College of Lore
- **Bonus Proficiencies (Level 3):** Gain proficiency in 3 additional skills.
- **Cutting Words (Level 3):** Reaction + Bardic Inspiration — subtract the die roll from an enemy's attack roll, damage roll, or ability check.
- **Magical Discoveries (Level 6):** Learn 2 spells from the Cleric, Druid, or Wizard list; always have them prepared.
- **Peerless Skill (Level 14):** Expend Bardic Inspiration after failing an ability check or attack roll to potentially turn it into a success (die not expended on continued failure).

#### College of Valor
- **Combat Inspiration (Level 3):** Creatures with your Bardic Inspiration can add it to their AC against one attack (Defense) or to a damage roll (Offense).
- **Martial Training (Level 3):** Proficiency with Martial weapons, Medium armor, and Shields. Can use a weapon as a spellcasting focus.
- **Extra Attack (Level 6):** Attack twice per Attack action; can replace one attack with a cantrip.
- **Battle Magic (Level 14):** After casting a spell as an action, make one weapon attack as a Bonus Action.
