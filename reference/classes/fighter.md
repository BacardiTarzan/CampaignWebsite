# FIGHTER

**Primary Ability:** Strength or Dexterity
**Hit Point Die:** d10
**Saving Throws:** Strength, Constitution
**Armor Training:** Light, Medium, Heavy armor, Shields
**Weapon Proficiencies:** Simple and Martial weapons
**Skill Proficiencies (choose 2):** Acrobatics, Animal Handling, Athletics, History, Insight, Intimidation, Persuasion, Perception, Survival
**Starting Equipment:** (A) Chain Mail, Greatsword, Flail, 8 Javelins, Dungeoneer's Pack, 4 GP; (B) Studded Leather, Scimitar, Shortsword, Longbow, 20 Arrows, Quiver, Dungeoneer's Pack, 11 GP; or (C) 155 GP

### Key Features

**Fighting Style (Level 1):** Gain a Fighting Style feat; can replace it on each level-up.

**Second Wind (Level 1):** Bonus Action — regain 1d10 + Fighter level HP. Uses scale with level; recharge 1 on Short Rest, all on Long Rest.

**Weapon Mastery (Level 1):** Use mastery properties of 3 weapons; change one on each Long Rest. Scales to 6 at level 16.

**Action Surge (Level 2):** Take one extra action (not Magic) on your turn; once per Short/Long Rest. Two uses per rest at level 17.

**Tactical Mind (Level 2):** Expend Second Wind to roll 1d10 and add it to a failed ability check (not expended if still fails).

**Extra Attack (Level 5):** Attack twice per Attack action. Three attacks at level 11, four at level 20.

**Tactical Shift (Level 5):** Move up to half Speed without provoking Opportunity Attacks when using Second Wind.

**Indomitable (Level 9):** Reroll a failed saving throw with a bonus equal to Fighter level; once per Long Rest. Scales to 2 uses at level 13, 3 at level 17.

**Tactical Master (Level 9):** Replace a weapon's mastery property with Push, Sap, or Slow for one attack.

**Studied Attacks (Level 13):** Missing an attack roll gives Advantage on the next roll against the same creature.

**Epic Boon (Level 19):** Gain an Epic Boon feat (Boon of Combat Prowess recommended).

### Subclasses

#### Battle Master
- **Combat Superiority (Level 3):** Learn 3 Maneuvers; gain 4 Superiority Dice (d8s). Add 2 maneuvers at levels 7, 10, 15; add dice at levels 7 (5) and 15 (6).
- **Student of War (Level 3):** Proficiency with one Artisan's Tool and one extra skill.
- **Know Your Enemy (Level 7):** Bonus Action — learn a creature's Immunities, Resistances, and Vulnerabilities. Once per Long Rest or expend a Superiority Die.
- **Improved Combat Superiority (Level 10):** Superiority Die becomes d10.
- **Relentless (Level 15):** Once per turn, roll 1d8 instead of expending a Superiority Die.
- **Ultimate Combat Superiority (Level 18):** Superiority Die becomes d12.

**Maneuvers (partial list):** Ambush, Bait and Switch, Commander's Strike, Commanding Presence, Disarming Attack, Distracting Strike, Evasive Footwork, Feinting Attack, Goading Attack, Lunging Attack, Maneuvering Attack, Menacing Attack, Parry, Precision Attack, Pushing Attack, Rally, Riposte, Sweeping Attack, Tactical Assessment, Trip Attack.

#### Champion
- **Improved Critical (Level 3):** Crit on 19–20.
- **Remarkable Athlete (Level 3):** Advantage on Initiative and Athletics checks; move half Speed after a crit.
- **Additional Fighting Style (Level 7):** Gain a second Fighting Style feat.
- **Heroic Warrior (Level 10):** Gain Heroic Inspiration at the start of your turn if you don't have it.
- **Superior Critical (Level 15):** Crit on 18–20.
- **Survivor (Level 18):** Advantage on Death Saving Throws (18–20 counts as 20). Regain HP = 5 + Con modifier at the start of turns when Bloodied.

#### Eldritch Knight
- **Spellcasting (Level 3):** Intelligence-based Wizard spells (mostly Abjuration/Evocation). Arcane Focus usable. Scaled spell slots (see Eldritch Knight table).
- **War Bond (Level 3):** Ritual-bond up to 2 weapons; cannot be disarmed; summon as Bonus Action. Can bond with magic weapons.
- **War Magic (Level 7):** Replace one attack with a Wizard cantrip.
- **Eldritch Strike (Level 10):** Weapon hits impose Disadvantage on the target's next save vs. your spell.
- **Arcane Charge (Level 15):** Teleport up to 30 ft. when using Action Surge.
- **Improved War Magic (Level 18):** Replace two attacks with a level 1 or 2 Wizard spell.

#### Psi Warrior
- **Psionic Power (Level 3):** Gain Psionic Energy Dice (d6, scaling to d12). Powers: Protective Field (reduce damage), Psionic Strike (bonus Force damage), Telekinetic Movement (move objects/creatures 30 ft.).
- **Telekinetic Adept (Level 7):** Psi-Powered Leap (Fly Speed = 2× Speed until end of turn); Telekinetic Thrust (push or prone on Psionic Strike).
- **Guarded Mind (Level 10):** Resistance to Psychic damage; expend Psionic die to end Charmed/Frightened.
- **Bulwark of Force (Level 15):** Grant Half Cover to chosen creatures within 30 ft. for 1 minute.
- **Telekinetic Master (Level 18):** Always have Telekinesis prepared; cast without a slot or components; make one weapon attack as Bonus Action each turn while concentrating on it.
