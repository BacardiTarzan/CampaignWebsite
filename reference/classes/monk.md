# MONK

**Primary Ability:** Dexterity and Wisdom
**Hit Point Die:** d8
**Saving Throws:** Strength, Dexterity
**Armor Training:** None
**Weapon Proficiencies:** Simple weapons and Martial weapons with the Light property
**Tool Proficiencies:** One Artisan's Tool or Musical Instrument
**Skill Proficiencies (choose 2):** Acrobatics, Athletics, History, Insight, Religion, Stealth
**Starting Equipment:** (A) Spear, 5 Daggers, Tool/Instrument, Explorer's Pack, 11 GP; or (B) 50 GP

### Key Features

**Martial Arts (Level 1):** While unarmed/using Monk weapons with no armor/Shield: Bonus Unarmed Strike, Martial Arts Die (scales to 1d12), can use Dexterity for attack/damage.

**Unarmored Defense (Level 1):** AC = 10 + Dex modifier + Wisdom modifier.

**Monk's Focus (Level 2):** Focus Points = Monk level. Powers: Flurry of Blows (1 FP — two Unarmed Strikes as Bonus Action), Patient Defense (Disengage free; or 1 FP for Disengage + Dodge), Step of the Wind (Dash free; or 1 FP for Disengage + Dash + doubled jump distance). Save DC = 8 + Wisdom modifier + Prof.

**Unarmored Movement (Level 2):** Speed bonus while unarmored/no Shield, scaling from +10 to +30 ft.

**Uncanny Metabolism (Level 2):** On rolling Initiative, regain all Focus Points and heal (Monk level + Martial Arts die) HP. Once per Long Rest.

**Deflect Attacks (Level 3):** Reaction — reduce Bludgeoning/Piercing/Slashing damage by 1d10 + Dex modifier + Monk level. If reduced to 0, spend 1 FP to redirect the attack at another creature.

**Extra Attack (Level 5):** Attack twice per Attack action.

**Stunning Strike (Level 5):** After hitting with a Monk weapon or Unarmed Strike, spend 1 FP — target makes Con save or is Stunned until your next turn; on success, target's Speed is halved and next attack roll against it has Advantage.

**Empowered Strikes (Level 6):** Unarmed Strikes deal Force damage or normal type.

**Evasion (Level 7):** No damage on successful Dexterity saves (half on failure).

**Acrobatic Movement (Level 9):** Run along vertical surfaces and across liquids during your turn.

**Heightened Focus (Level 10):** Flurry of Blows grants 3 strikes (1 FP). Patient Defense grants Temp HP. Step of the Wind can move a willing creature.

**Self-Restoration (Level 10):** Remove one of Charmed, Frightened, or Poisoned at the end of each turn.

**Deflect Energy (Level 13):** Deflect Attacks now works against any damage type.

**Disciplined Survivor (Level 14):** Proficiency in all saving throws; can spend 1 FP to reroll a failed save.

**Perfect Focus (Level 15):** If not using Uncanny Metabolism on Initiative, regain FP up to 4 if you have fewer than 4.

**Superior Defense (Level 18):** Spend 3 FP at start of your turn — gain Resistance to all damage except Force for 1 minute.

**Epic Boon (Level 19):** Gain an Epic Boon feat (Boon of Irresistible Offense recommended).

**Body and Mind (Level 20):** Dexterity and Wisdom scores increase by 4 (max 25).

### Subclasses

#### Warrior of Mercy
- **Hand of Harm (Level 3):** Once per turn after hitting with Unarmed Strike, spend 1 FP to deal +1 Martial Arts die + Wisdom modifier Necrotic damage.
- **Hand of Healing (Level 3):** Magic Action + 1 FP — touch a creature to heal 1 Martial Arts die + Wisdom modifier HP. Can replace a Flurry of Blows strike with this for free.
- **Implements of Mercy (Level 3):** Gain Insight, Medicine, and Herbalism Kit proficiency.
- **Physician's Touch (Level 6):** Hand of Harm can Poison the target; Hand of Healing can end one condition (Blinded, Deafened, Paralyzed, Poisoned, or Stunned).
- **Flurry of Healing and Harm (Level 11):** Replace Flurry strikes with Hand of Healing/Harm freely (limited by Wisdom modifier uses per Long Rest).
- **Hand of Ultimate Mercy (Level 17):** Touch a corpse dead ≤24 hours, spend 5 FP — revive it with 4d10 + Wisdom modifier HP. Once per Long Rest.

#### Warrior of Shadow
- **Shadow Arts (Level 3):** Spend 1 FP to cast Darkness (can see within it, can relocate it). Gain 60-ft. Darkvision. Know Minor Illusion (Wisdom spellcasting).
- **Shadow Step (Level 6):** Bonus Action — teleport 60 ft. between dim/dark areas; Advantage on next melee attack.
- **Improved Shadow Step (Level 11):** Spend 1 FP to remove the dim/dark requirement; make an Unarmed Strike as part of the teleport.
- **Cloak of Shadows (Level 17):** Magic Action + 3 FP — become Invisible and partially incorporeal for 1 minute while in dim/dark; use Flurry of Blows without FP.

#### Warrior of the Elements
- **Elemental Attunement (Level 3):** Start of your turn, spend 1 FP — gain elemental powers for 10 minutes: reach +10 ft. on Unarmed Strikes, and deal Acid/Cold/Fire/Lightning/Thunder damage + push/pull target 10 ft. (Strength save).
- **Manipulate Elements (Level 3):** Know Elementalism spell (Wisdom spellcasting).
- **Elemental Burst (Level 6):** Magic Action + 2 FP — elemental burst in 20-ft. Sphere within 120 ft. (Dexterity save; 3× Martial Arts die damage).
- **Stride of the Elements (Level 11):** While Attuned, also gain Fly and Swim Speeds = Speed.
- **Elemental Epitome (Level 17):** While Attuned: Resistance to one elemental damage type; Destructive Stride (speed +20 ft., deal damage to creatures you pass near); Empowered Strikes (extra Martial Arts die on Unarmed hit once per turn).

#### Warrior of the Open Hand
- **Open Hand Technique (Level 3):** After hitting with a Flurry of Blows attack, impose Addle (no Opportunity Attacks), Push (Strength save or pushed 15 ft.), or Topple (Dexterity save or Prone).
- **Wholeness of Body (Level 6):** Bonus Action — heal yourself for Martial Arts die + Wisdom modifier HP. Uses = Wisdom modifier; recharge on Long Rest.
- **Fleet Step (Level 11):** When taking a Bonus Action other than Step of the Wind, also use Step of the Wind.
- **Quivering Palm (Level 17):** After hitting with Unarmed Strike, spend 4 FP — set up lethal vibrations lasting Monk level days. Action to end them: target makes Con save or takes 10d12 Force damage.
