# RANGER

**Primary Ability:** Dexterity and Wisdom
**Hit Point Die:** d10
**Saving Throws:** Strength, Dexterity
**Armor Training:** Light, Medium armor, Shields
**Weapon Proficiencies:** Simple and Martial weapons
**Skill Proficiencies (choose 3):** Animal Handling, Athletics, Insight, Investigation, Nature, Perception, Stealth, Survival
**Starting Equipment:** (A) Studded Leather, Scimitar, Shortsword, Longbow, 20 Arrows, Quiver, Druidic Focus, Explorer's Pack, 7 GP; or (B) 150 GP

### Key Features

**Spellcasting (Level 1):** Wisdom-based. Use a Druidic Focus. Change one prepared spell on each Long Rest.

**Favored Enemy (Level 1):** Always have Hunter's Mark prepared; cast it without a slot (uses scale with level).

**Weapon Mastery (Level 1):** Use mastery properties of 2 proficient weapons; change on Long Rest.

**Deft Explorer (Level 2):** Gain Expertise in one skill. Know two extra languages.

**Fighting Style (Level 2):** Gain a Fighting Style feat; optionally choose Druidic Warrior (two Druid cantrips, Wisdom-based).

**Extra Attack (Level 5):** Attack twice per Attack action.

**Roving (Level 6):** Speed +10 ft. (not Heavy armor); gain Climb and Swim Speeds = Speed.

**Expertise (Level 9):** Gain Expertise in two more skill proficiencies.

**Tireless (Level 10):** Gain Temp HP as a Magic Action (1d8 + Wisdom modifier; uses = Wisdom modifier). Short Rests reduce Exhaustion by 1.

**Relentless Hunter (Level 13):** Damage cannot break Concentration on Hunter's Mark.

**Nature's Veil (Level 14):** Bonus Action — gain Invisible condition until end of next turn. Uses = Wisdom modifier; recharge on Long Rest.

**Precise Hunter (Level 17):** Advantage on attack rolls against creatures marked by Hunter's Mark.

**Feral Senses (Level 18):** Gain Blindsight 30 ft.

**Epic Boon (Level 19):** Gain an Epic Boon feat (Boon of Dimensional Travel recommended).

**Foe Slayer (Level 20):** Hunter's Mark damage die is now d10.

### Subclasses

#### Beast Master
- **Primal Companion (Level 3):** Summon a Beast of the Land, Sea, or Sky. It acts on your turn (Dodge unless commanded); command it as a Bonus Action to take an action or sacrifice an attack.
- **Exceptional Training (Level 7):** Companion can Dash, Disengage, Dodge, or Help as a Bonus Action. Its damage can be Force type.
- **Bestial Fury (Level 11):** Beast's Strike can be used twice. Against Hunter's Mark targets, it deals extra Force damage.
- **Share Spells (Level 15):** Self-targeting spells also affect your companion within 30 ft.

#### Fey Wanderer
- **Dreadful Strikes (Level 3):** Weapon hits deal +1d4 Psychic damage (once per turn; scales to 1d6 at level 11).
- **Fey Wanderer Spells (Level 3):** Charm Person (3), Misty Step (5), Summon Fey (9), Dimension Door (13), Mislead (17). Also receive a Feywild Gift.
- **Otherworldly Glamour (Level 3):** Add Wisdom modifier to Charisma checks; gain proficiency in Deception, Performance, or Persuasion.
- **Beguiling Twist (Level 7):** Advantage on saves vs. Charmed/Frightened; Reaction to redirect such a condition to another creature when you or nearby ally succeeds.
- **Fey Reinforcements (Level 11):** Cast Summon Fey without material components; cast it once without a slot per Long Rest; can make it non-Concentration (lasts 1 minute).
- **Misty Wanderer (Level 15):** Cast Misty Step without a slot (uses = Wisdom modifier); can bring one willing creature along.

#### Gloom Stalker
- **Dread Ambusher (Level 3):** First turn: Speed +10 ft. Dreadful Strike (extra 2d6 Psychic once per turn; uses = Wisdom modifier). Initiative bonus = Wisdom modifier.
- **Gloom Stalker Spells (Level 3):** Disguise Self (3), Rope Trick (5), Fear (9), Greater Invisibility (13), Seeming (17).
- **Umbral Sight (Level 3):** Gain Darkvision 60 ft. (or +60 ft.). While in Darkness, Invisible to creatures relying on Darkvision.
- **Iron Mind (Level 7):** Proficiency in Wisdom saves (or Intelligence/Charisma saves if already have Wisdom).
- **Stalker's Flurry (Level 11):** Dreadful Strike damage becomes 2d8; can cause Sudden Strike (extra attack on adjacent foe) or Mass Fear (Wisdom save or Frightened).
- **Shadowy Dodge (Level 15):** Reaction to impose Disadvantage on an incoming attack; then teleport 30 ft.

#### Hunter
- **Hunter's Lore (Level 3):** Learn the Immunities, Resistances, and Vulnerabilities of creatures marked by Hunter's Mark.
- **Hunter's Prey (Level 3):** Choose Colossus Slayer (extra 1d8 damage to Bloodied creatures once per turn) or Horde Breaker (extra attack vs. adjacent foe once per turn). Swap on Short/Long Rest.
- **Defensive Tactics (Level 7):** Choose Escape the Horde (Disadvantage on Opportunity Attacks against you) or Multiattack Defense (Disadvantage on further attacks after first hit). Swap on Short/Long Rest.
- **Superior Hunter's Prey (Level 11):** Once per turn when dealing Hunter's Mark damage, also deal that extra damage to a different creature within 30 ft.
- **Superior Hunter's Defense (Level 15):** Reaction to gain Resistance to damage you just took (and same type) until end of current turn.
