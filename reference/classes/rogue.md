# ROGUE

**Primary Ability:** Dexterity
**Hit Point Die:** d8
**Saving Throws:** Dexterity, Intelligence
**Armor Training:** Light armor
**Weapon Proficiencies:** Simple weapons and Martial weapons with Finesse or Light property
**Tool Proficiencies:** Thieves' Tools
**Skill Proficiencies (choose 4):** Acrobatics, Athletics, Deception, Insight, Intimidation, Investigation, Perception, Persuasion, Sleight of Hand, Stealth
**Starting Equipment:** (A) Leather Armor, 2 Daggers, Shortsword, Shortbow, 20 Arrows, Quiver, Thieves' Tools, Burglar's Pack, 8 GP; or (B) 100 GP

### Key Features

**Expertise (Level 1):** Gain Expertise in 2 skill proficiencies; 2 more at level 6.

**Sneak Attack (Level 1):** Once per turn, deal extra damage with Finesse or Ranged weapon when you have Advantage, or when an ally is adjacent to target (no Disadvantage required).

**Thieves' Cant (Level 1):** Know Thieves' Cant and one extra language.

**Weapon Mastery (Level 1):** Use mastery properties of 2 proficient weapons; change on Long Rest.

**Cunning Action (Level 2):** Bonus Action — Dash, Disengage, or Hide.

**Steady Aim (Level 3):** Bonus Action — give yourself Advantage on your next attack (requires no movement; Speed becomes 0).

**Cunning Strike (Level 5):** After dealing Sneak Attack damage, add an effect by removing dice from the total (DC = 8 + Dex modifier + Prof.): Poison (1d6 cost — Poisoned for 1 minute), Trip (1d6 — Prone if Large or smaller), Withdraw (1d6 — move half Speed safely).

**Uncanny Dodge (Level 5):** Reaction — halve one attack's damage.

**Evasion (Level 7):** No damage on successful Dexterity saves; half on failure.

**Reliable Talent (Level 7):** Treat d20 rolls of 9 or lower as 10 for proficient skill/tool checks.

**Improved Cunning Strike (Level 11):** Use up to two Cunning Strike effects at once.

**Devious Strikes (Level 14):** New Cunning Strike options: Daze (2d6 — Constitution save or highly restricted on next turn), Knock Out (6d6 — Unconscious for 1 minute), Obscure (3d6 — Blinded until end of next turn).

**Slippery Mind (Level 15):** Proficiency in Wisdom and Charisma saving throws.

**Elusive (Level 18):** Attack rolls against you can never have Advantage (unless Incapacitated).

**Epic Boon (Level 19):** Gain an Epic Boon feat (Boon of the Night Spirit recommended).

**Stroke of Luck (Level 20):** Turn a failed D20 Test into a 20; once per Short or Long Rest.

### Subclasses

#### Arcane Trickster
- **Spellcasting (Level 3):** Intelligence-based Wizard spells. Know Mage Hand + 2 cantrips; spells scale per Arcane Trickster table.
- **Mage Hand Legerdemain (Level 3):** Cast Mage Hand as Bonus Action; make the hand Invisible; use it for Sleight of Hand checks as Bonus Action.
- **Magical Ambush (Level 9):** Targets have Disadvantage on saves against your spells when you cast while Invisible.
- **Versatile Trickster (Level 13):** When Tripping with Cunning Strike, can also Trip another creature within 5 ft. of your Mage Hand.
- **Spell Thief (Level 17):** Reaction after being targeted by a spell — force the caster to make an Intelligence save or you negate the effect and steal the spell for 8 hours.

#### Assassin
- **Assassinate (Level 3):** Advantage on Initiative rolls. During first round of combat, Advantage on attacks vs. creatures that haven't acted; Sneak Attack hits deal extra damage = Rogue level.
- **Assassin's Tools (Level 3):** Gain a Disguise Kit and Poisoner's Kit with proficiency.
- **Infiltration Expertise (Level 9):** Can perfectly mimic another's speech/handwriting. Steady Aim no longer reduces Speed.
- **Envenom Weapons (Level 13):** Poison Cunning Strike also deals 2d6 Poison damage on each failed save (ignores Resistance).
- **Death Strike (Level 17):** First Sneak Attack hit in a combat forces a Con save or doubles the damage.

#### Soulknife
- **Psionic Power (Level 3):** Psionic Energy Dice (d6, scaling to d12). Powers: Psi-Bolstered Knack (roll die to save a failed proficient skill check), Psychic Whispers (Magic Action — establish telepathy with creatures for hours).
- **Psychic Blades (Level 3):** On Attack action or Opportunity Attacks, manifest a blade (1d6 Psychic, Finesse, Thrown 60/120 ft.). Make a Bonus Action second attack (1d4).
- **Soul Blades (Level 9):** Homing Strikes (expend die to add to a missed blade attack); Psychic Teleportation (Bonus Action — throw blade and teleport to landing spot).
- **Psychic Veil (Level 13):** Magic Action — become Invisible for 1 hour; ends on dealing damage or forcing a save. Once per Long Rest or expend a die.
- **Rend Mind (Level 17):** When dealing Sneak Attack with Psychic Blades, target makes Wisdom save or is Stunned for 1 minute. Once per Long Rest or expend 3 dice.

#### Thief
- **Fast Hands (Level 3):** Bonus Action — make a Sleight of Hand check (pick lock, disarm trap, pick pocket) or take the Utilize/Magic action.
- **Second-Story Work (Level 3):** Climb Speed = Speed; use Dexterity instead of Strength for jump distance.
- **Supreme Sneak (Level 9):** Cunning Strike option: Stealth Attack (1d6 — remain Invisible after attack if ending turn in cover).
- **Use Magic Device (Level 13):** Attune to 4 items at once; on rolling 6 on d6, use charged item property without expending charges; use any Spell Scroll (Intelligence-based).
- **Thief's Reflexes (Level 17):** Take two turns in the first round of combat (second turn at Initiative − 10).
