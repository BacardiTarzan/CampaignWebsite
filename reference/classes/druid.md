### Key Features

**Druidic (Level 1):** Know the secret language of Druids; always have Speak with Animals prepared; can leave hidden messages.

**Primal Order (Level 1):** Choose Magician (extra cantrip + Wisdom modifier to Arcana/Nature checks) or Warden (Martial weapons + Medium armor).

**Wild Shape (Level 2):** Bonus Action — transform into a known Beast form for hours = half Druid level, or until Incapacitated or choosing to end it. Gain Temp HP = Druid level.

**Wild Companion (Level 2):** Magic Action — expend a spell slot or Wild Shape use to cast Find Familiar without material components (familiar is Fey, disappears on Long Rest).

**Wild Resurgence (Level 5):** Give yourself one Wild Shape use by expending a spell slot (once per turn). Or expend a Wild Shape use for a level 1 spell slot (once per Long Rest).

**Elemental Fury (Level 7):** Choose Potent Spellcasting (add Wisdom modifier to cantrip damage) or Primal Strike (extra 1d8 elemental damage on weapon/Wild Shape attack once per turn).

**Improved Elemental Fury (Level 15):** Potent Spellcasting: range +300 ft. for 10+ ft. range cantrips. Primal Strike: increases to 2d8.

**Beast Spells (Level 18):** Cast spells while in Wild Shape form (except spells with costly material components).

**Epic Boon (Level 19):** Gain an Epic Boon feat (Boon of Dimensional Travel recommended).

**Archdruid (Level 20):** Regain one Wild Shape use when rolling Initiative with none remaining. Convert Wild Shape uses into spell slots (2 uses per spell level). Age 10× more slowly.

### Subclasses

#### Circle of the Land
- **Circle of the Land Spells (Level 3):** On each Long Rest, choose Arid, Polar, Temperate, or Tropical land; gain bonus prepared spells matching that terrain.
- **Land's Aid (Level 3):** Expend Wild Shape — deal 2d6 Necrotic in 10-ft. Sphere and heal one creature for 2d6 HP. Scales to 3d6/4d6 at levels 10/14.
- **Natural Recovery (Level 6):** Cast one Circle Spell without a slot (once per Long Rest); recover spell slots on Short Rest (combined level ≤ half Druid level, no level 6+).
- **Nature's Ward (Level 10):** Immune to Poisoned; Resistance to one damage type based on current land choice.
- **Nature's Sanctuary (Level 14):** Expend Wild Shape — create a 15-ft. Cube of spectral trees granting Half Cover (moveable as Bonus Action) and allies share your Nature's Ward Resistance.

#### Circle of the Moon
- **Circle Forms (Level 3):** Wild Shape max CR = Druid level ÷ 3; AC = max(Beast AC, 13 + Wisdom modifier); Temp HP = 3× Druid level.
- **Circle of the Moon Spells (Level 3):** Cure Wounds, Moonbeam, Starry Wisp (3); Conjure Animals (5); Fount of Moonlight (7); Mass Cure Wounds (9). Can cast these while shape-shifted.
- **Improved Circle Forms (Level 6):** Each attack in Wild Shape can deal Radiant damage; add Wisdom modifier to Con saves.
- **Moonlight Step (Level 10):** Bonus Action — teleport 30 ft. with Advantage on next attack. Uses = Wisdom modifier; recharge on Long Rest or by expending level 2+ spell slots.
- **Lunar Form (Level 14):** Once per turn deal +2d10 Radiant on Wild Shape attack; can share Moonlight Step teleport with one willing creature.

#### Circle of the Sea
- **Circle of the Sea Spells (Level 3):** Fog Cloud, Gust of Wind, Ray of Frost, Shatter, Thunderwave (3); Lightning Bolt, Water Breathing (5); Control Water, Ice Storm (7); Conjure Elemental, Hold Monster (9).
- **Wrath of the Sea (Level 3):** Expend Wild Shape — create 5-ft. Emanation for 10 min. that deals Cold damage and pushes creatures (Con save vs. spell save DC).
- **Aquatic Affinity (Level 6):** Emanation grows to 10 ft.; gain Swim Speed = Speed.
- **Stormborn (Level 10):** While Wrath of the Sea is active, gain Fly Speed = Speed and Resistance to Cold, Lightning, Thunder.
- **Oceanic Gift (Level 14):** Manifest Wrath of the Sea around another willing creature within 60 ft. instead of yourself; can affect both by expending 2 Wild Shape uses.

#### Circle of the Stars
- **Star Map (Level 3):** Create a star chart as a Spellcasting Focus; always have Guidance and Guiding Bolt prepared; can cast Guiding Bolt without a slot (uses = Wisdom modifier).
- **Starry Form (Level 3):** Expend Wild Shape — take on a glowing starry form for 10 minutes (doesn't transform shape). Choose Archer (bonus ranged Radiant attack), Chalice (heal when casting healing spell), or Dragon (treat d20 roll of ≤9 as 10 for Int/Wis checks and Con saves).
- **Cosmic Omen (Level 6):** Roll a die after Long Rest; gain a Weal (add 1d6 to D20 Tests) or Woe (subtract 1d6) Reaction. Uses = Wisdom modifier.
- **Twinkling Constellations (Level 10):** Archer and Chalice dice become 2d8; Dragon grants 20-ft. Fly Speed. Can change constellation at start of each turn.
- **Full of Stars (Level 14):** In Starry Form, become partially incorporeal; gain Resistance to Bludgeoning, Piercing, Slashing.
