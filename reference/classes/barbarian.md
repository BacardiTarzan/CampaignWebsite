# BARBARIAN

**Primary Ability:** Strength
**Hit Point Die:** d12
**Saving Throws:** Strength, Constitution
**Armor Training:** Light, Medium armor, Shields
**Weapon Proficiencies:** Simple and Martial weapons
**Skill Proficiencies (choose 2):** Animal Handling, Athletics, Intimidation, Nature, Perception, Survival
**Starting Equipment:** (A) Greataxe, 4 Handaxes, Explorer's Pack, 15 GP; or (B) 75 GP

### Key Features

**Rage (Level 1):** Enter as a Bonus Action (not while wearing Heavy armor). Grants Resistance to Bludgeoning/Piercing/Slashing damage, bonus Rage Damage on Strength attacks, Advantage on Strength checks and saves. Cannot concentrate or cast spells. Lasts until end of next turn; extend each round by attacking, forcing a save, or using a Bonus Action. Max 10 minutes.

**Unarmored Defense (Level 1):** AC = 10 + Dex modifier + Con modifier (can still use a Shield).

**Weapon Mastery (Level 1):** Use mastery properties of 2 Simple/Martial Melee weapons; increases at higher levels.

**Danger Sense (Level 2):** Advantage on Dexterity saving throws (unless Incapacitated).

**Reckless Attack (Level 2):** Gain Advantage on Strength attack rolls until next turn, but attacks against you also have Advantage.

**Primal Knowledge (Level 3):** Gain an extra skill proficiency. While Raging, can make Acrobatics, Intimidation, Perception, Stealth, or Survival checks as Strength checks.

**Extra Attack (Level 5):** Attack twice per Attack action.

**Fast Movement (Level 5):** Speed +10 ft. (not in Heavy armor).

**Feral Instinct (Level 7):** Advantage on Initiative rolls.

**Instinctive Pounce (Level 7):** Move up to half Speed as part of the Bonus Action to enter Rage.

**Brutal Strike (Level 9):** When using Reckless Attack, forgo Advantage on one Strength attack to deal +1d10 damage and apply a Brutal Strike effect (Forceful Blow or Hamstring Blow).

**Relentless Rage (Level 11):** On dropping to 0 HP during Rage, make DC 10 Con save to survive with HP = 2× Barbarian level. DC increases by 5 each use; resets on Short/Long Rest.

**Improved Brutal Strike (Level 13):** Gain Staggering Blow and Sundering Blow options.

**Persistent Rage (Level 15):** Roll Initiative to regain all Rage uses (once per Long Rest). Rage now lasts 10 minutes without extension; ends only on Unconscious or Heavy armor.

**Improved Brutal Strike (Level 17):** Extra damage increases to 2d10; use two Brutal Strike effects at once.

**Indomitable Might (Level 18):** Strength checks/saves never fall below your Strength score.

**Epic Boon (Level 19):** Gain an Epic Boon feat (Boon of Irresistible Offense recommended).

**Primal Champion (Level 20):** Strength and Constitution scores increase by 4 (max 25).

### Subclasses

#### Path of the Berserker
- **Frenzy (Level 3):** Using Reckless Attack while Raging, deal extra damage on first hit = [Rage Damage bonus]d6.
- **Mindless Rage (Level 6):** Immunity to Charmed and Frightened while Raging.
- **Retaliation (Level 10):** Reaction to make a melee attack when a creature within 5 ft. damages you.
- **Intimidating Presence (Level 14):** Bonus Action — creatures in 30-ft. Emanation make Wisdom save (DC 8 + Str mod + Prof.) or become Frightened for 1 minute.

#### Path of the Wild Heart
- **Animal Speaker (Level 3):** Cast Beast Sense and Speak with Animals as Rituals (Wisdom spellcasting).
- **Rage of the Wilds (Level 3):** Choose Bear (broad Resistance), Eagle (Disengage/Dash options), or Wolf (allies gain Advantage vs. nearby enemies) when activating Rage.
- **Aspect of the Wilds (Level 6):** Choose Owl (Darkvision +60 ft.), Panther (Climb Speed), or Salmon (Swim Speed); change on Long Rest.
- **Nature Speaker (Level 10):** Cast Commune with Nature as a Ritual.
- **Power of the Wilds (Level 14):** Choose Falcon (Fly Speed), Lion (enemies near you have Disadvantage vs. others), or Ram (knock prone on melee hit).

#### Path of the World Tree
- **Vitality of the Tree (Level 3):** Gain Temp HP = Barbarian level on activating Rage; give Temp HP to nearby creatures each turn.
- **Branches of the Tree (Level 6):** Reaction — teleport a creature that starts its turn within 30 ft. of you to within 5 ft. of you (Strength save to resist).
- **Battering Roots (Level 10):** Melee reach +10 ft. with Heavy/Versatile weapons; can activate Push or Topple mastery in addition to another mastery.
- **Travel Along the Tree (Level 14):** Teleport up to 60 ft. on activating Rage and as a Bonus Action; once per Rage, extend to 150 ft. and bring up to 6 willing creatures.

#### Path of the Zealot
- **Divine Fury (Level 3):** First hit each turn while Raging deals +1d6 + half Barbarian level Necrotic or Radiant damage.
- **Warrior of the Gods (Level 3):** Pool of 4d12s to heal yourself as a Bonus Action; grows to 7d12 at level 17.
- **Fanatical Focus (Level 6):** Once per Rage, reroll a failed saving throw with a bonus equal to Rage Damage bonus.
- **Zealous Presence (Level 10):** Bonus Action — up to 10 allies within 60 ft. gain Advantage on attacks and saves until your next turn.
- **Rage of the Gods (Level 14):** Activate Rage to assume divine warrior form for 1 minute: Fly Speed, Resistance to Necrotic/Psychic/Radiant, and Revivification (spend a Rage use as Reaction to save a nearby creature dropping to 0 HP).
