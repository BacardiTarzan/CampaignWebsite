### Key Features

**Eldritch Invocations (Level 1):** Gain supernatural abilities. Total invocations scale with level. Can replace one per level-up.

**Pact Magic (Level 1):** Charisma-based. All spell slots are the same level (scales 1→5); regain all on Short or Long Rest. Use an Arcane Focus.

**Magical Cunning (Level 2):** Perform an esoteric rite for 1 minute — regain up to half max Pact Magic slots (round up). Once per Long Rest.

**Contact Patron (Level 7/9 feature at level 9 in table):** Always have Contact Other Plane prepared; cast it to speak with patron without expending a slot (auto-succeed on save). Once per Long Rest.

**Mystic Arcanum (Level 11):** Choose one level 6 Warlock spell; cast it once without a slot per Long Rest. Gain level 7 at level 13, level 8 at level 15, level 9 at level 17.

**Epic Boon (Level 19):** Gain an Epic Boon feat (Boon of Fate recommended).

**Eldritch Master (Level 20):** Magical Cunning now restores all expended Pact Magic slots.

### Subclasses

#### Archfey Patron
*Spells:* Calm Emotions, Faerie Fire, Misty Step, Phantasmal Force, Sleep (3); Blink, Plant Growth (5); Dominate Beast, Greater Invisibility (7); Dominate Person, Seeming (9).
- **Steps of the Fey (Level 3):** Cast Misty Step without a slot (uses = Cha modifier); choose an additional effect: Refreshing Step (Temp HP) or Taunting Step (Disadvantage on others' attacks).
- **Misty Escape (Level 6):** Reaction to cast Misty Step when taking damage. New options: Disappearing Step (Invisible until next turn) and Dreadful Step (Psychic damage near origin/destination).
- **Beguiling Defenses (Level 10):** Immune to Charmed; Reaction to halve damage and deal matching Psychic damage to attacker.
- **Bewitching Magic (Level 14):** After casting an Enchantment/Illusion spell as an action, cast Misty Step for free as part of that action.

#### Celestial Patron
*Spells:* Aid, Cure Wounds, Guiding Bolt, Lesser Restoration, Light, Sacred Flame (3); Daylight, Revivify (5); Guardian of Faith, Wall of Fire (7); Greater Restoration, Summon Celestial (9).
- **Healing Light (Level 3):** Bonus Action — heal using a pool of d6s = 1 + Warlock level (max = Cha modifier dice at once); recharge on Long Rest.
- **Radiant Soul (Level 6):** Resistance to Radiant damage; add Cha modifier to one Radiant or Fire damage roll per turn.
- **Celestial Resilience (Level 10):** Gain Temp HP = Warlock level + Cha modifier on Short/Long Rest or Magical Cunning; up to 5 chosen creatures gain half that.
- **Searing Vengeance (Level 14):** When an ally within 60 ft. would make a Death Save, restore half their max HP; nearby enemies take 2d8 + Cha modifier Radiant damage and are Blinded.

#### Fiend Patron
*Spells:* Burning Hands, Command, Scorching Ray, Suggestion (3); Fireball, Stinking Cloud (5); Fire Shield, Wall of Fire (7); Geas, Insect Plague (9).
- **Dark One's Blessing (Level 3):** Gain Temp HP = Cha modifier + Warlock level when you or a nearby ally reduce an enemy to 0 HP.
- **Dark One's Own Luck (Level 6):** Add 1d10 to one ability check or saving throw (after seeing the roll); uses = Cha modifier per Long Rest.
- **Fiendish Resilience (Level 10):** Choose a damage type on each Short/Long Rest — gain Resistance to it.
- **Hurl Through Hell (Level 14):** Once per turn on a hit — target makes Charisma save or vanishes through the Lower Planes for 1 round, taking 8d10 Psychic damage (non-Fiends) and returning Incapacitated. Once per Long Rest or expend a Pact slot.

#### Great Old One Patron
*Spells:* Detect Thoughts, Dissonant Whispers, Phantasmal Force, Tasha's Hideous Laughter (3); Clairvoyance, Hunger of Hadar (5); Confusion, Summon Aberration (7); Modify Memory, Telekinesis (9).
- **Awakened Mind (Level 3):** Bonus Action — establish telepathy with one creature for Warlock level minutes.
- **Psychic Spells (Level 3):** Change any Warlock spell's damage type to Psychic; cast Enchantment/Illusion Warlock spells without V/S components.
- **Clairvoyant Combatant (Level 6):** Force telepathically bonded creature to make Wisdom save — Disadvantage vs. you, Advantage for you. Once per Short/Long Rest or Pact slot.
- **Eldritch Hex (Level 10):** Always have Hex prepared; when you cast Hex, the target also has Disadvantage on saves of the chosen ability.
- **Thought Shield (Level 10):** Mind cannot be read; Resistance to Psychic damage; creature dealing Psychic damage to you takes the same amount.
- **Create Thrall (Level 14):** When casting Summon Aberration, make it non-Concentration (lasts 1 minute) with bonus Temp HP; first hit per turn on Hexed creatures deals extra Psychic damage.
