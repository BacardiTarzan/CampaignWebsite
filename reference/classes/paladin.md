# PALADIN

**Hit Point Die:** d10
**Saving Throws:** Wisdom, Charisma
**Armor Training:** Light, Medium, Heavy armor, Shields
**Weapon Proficiencies:** Simple and Martial weapons

**Starting Equipment:** (A) Chain Mail, Shield, Longsword, 6 Javelins, Holy Symbol, Priest's Pack, 9 GP; or (B) 150 GP

### Key Features

**Lay On Hands (Level 1):** Pool = 5× Paladin level. Bonus Action — restore HP to a creature from the pool; or expend 5 HP to remove Poisoned.

**Spellcasting (Level 1):** Charisma-based. Change one prepared spell on each Long Rest. Use Holy Symbol as focus.

**Weapon Mastery (Level 1):** Use mastery properties of 2 proficient weapons; change on Long Rest.

**Fighting Style (Level 2):** Gain a Fighting Style feat; can optionally choose Blessed Warrior (two Cleric cantrips, Charisma-based).

**Paladin's Smite (Level 2):** Always have Divine Smite prepared; cast it once without a spell slot per Long Rest.

**Channel Divinity (Level 3):** 2 uses; regain 1 on Short Rest, all on Long Rest. Divine Sense: Bonus Action — detect Celestials, Fiends, and Undead within 60 ft. for 10 minutes.

**Extra Attack (Level 5):** Attack twice per Attack action.

**Faithful Steed (Level 5):** Always have Find Steed prepared; cast once without a slot per Long Rest.

**Aura of Protection (Level 6):** You and allies within 10 ft. gain Charisma modifier bonus (min +1) to saving throws. Inactive if Incapacitated.

**Abjure Foes (Level 9):** Magic Action + Channel Divinity — Frighten up to Charisma modifier creatures within 60 ft. (Wisdom save); Frightened creatures are highly restricted each turn.

**Aura of Courage (Level 10):** You and allies in Aura of Protection are immune to Frightened.

**Radiant Strikes (Level 11):** Melee weapon hits deal +1d8 Radiant damage.

**Restoring Touch (Level 14):** When using Lay On Hands, also remove conditions (5 HP per condition): Blinded, Charmed, Deafened, Frightened, Paralyzed, or Stunned.

**Aura Expansion (Level 18):** Aura of Protection extends to 30 ft.

**Epic Boon (Level 19):** Gain an Epic Boon feat (Boon of Truesight recommended).

### Subclasses

#### Oath of Devotion
*Oath Spells:* Protection from Evil and Good, Shield of Faith (3); Aid, Zone of Truth (5); Beacon of Hope, Dispel Magic (9); Freedom of Movement, Guardian of Faith (13); Commune, Flame Strike (17).
- **Sacred Weapon (Level 3):** Channel Divinity — one melee weapon gains +Cha modifier to attack rolls and deals Radiant damage for 10 minutes; emits Bright Light.
- **Aura of Devotion (Level 7):** Immunity to Charmed in Aura of Protection.
- **Smite of Protection (Level 15):** Casting Divine Smite grants Half Cover to aura allies until your next turn.
- **Holy Nimbus (Level 20):** Bonus Action — Advantage on saves vs. Fiends/Undead; enemies in aura take Cha modifier + Prof. Radiant damage per turn; aura filled with sunlight. Lasts 10 minutes.

#### Oath of Glory
*Oath Spells:* Guiding Bolt, Heroism (3); Enhance Ability, Magic Weapon (5); Haste, Protection from Energy (9); Compulsion, Freedom of Movement (13); Legend Lore, Yolande's Regal Presence (17).
- **Inspiring Smite (Level 3):** Channel Divinity after casting Divine Smite — distribute 2d8 + Paladin level Temp HP among creatures within 30 ft.
- **Peerless Athlete (Level 3):** Channel Divinity — Advantage on Athletics/Acrobatics for 1 hour; jump distance +10 ft.
- **Aura of Alacrity (Level 7):** Your Speed +10 ft.; allies entering the aura or starting turns there gain +10 ft. Speed until their next turn.
- **Glorious Defense (Level 15):** Reaction — add Cha modifier to an ally's AC vs. one attack; if it misses, make a weapon attack as part of the Reaction.
- **Living Legend (Level 20):** Bonus Action for 10 minutes — Advantage on Charisma checks; reroll failed saves as Reaction; once per turn, turn a missed attack into a hit.

#### Oath of the Ancients
*Oath Spells:* Ensnaring Strike, Speak with Animals (3); Misty Step, Moonbeam (5); Plant Growth, Protection from Energy (9); Ice Storm, Stoneskin (13); Commune with Nature, Tree Stride (17).
- **Nature's Wrath (Level 3):** Channel Divinity — Restrain creatures within 15 ft. (Strength save; repeat each turn).
- **Aura of Warding (Level 7):** Resistance to Necrotic, Psychic, Radiant damage in Aura of Protection.
- **Undying Sentinel (Level 15):** When dropped to 0 HP, drop to 1 HP and heal 3× Paladin level instead. Cannot be aged magically.
- **Elder Champion (Level 20):** Bonus Action for 1 minute — enemies in aura have Disadvantage on saves; regain 10 HP at start of each turn; cast action spells as Bonus Actions.

#### Oath of Vengeance
*Oath Spells:* Bane, Hunter's Mark (3); Hold Person, Misty Step (5); Haste, Protection from Energy (9); Banishment, Dimension Door (13); Hold Monster, Scrying (17).
- **Vow of Enmity (Level 3):** Channel Divinity — Advantage on attack rolls against one creature within 30 ft. for 1 minute; can transfer vow on target's death.
- **Relentless Avenger (Level 7):** On an Opportunity Attack hit, reduce target's Speed to 0; move half your Speed as part of the same Reaction.
- **Soul of Vengeance (Level 15):** Reaction — make a melee attack when the vowed creature hits or misses with an attack.
- **Avenging Angel (Level 20):** Bonus Action for 10 minutes — gain 60-ft. Fly Speed; enemies starting turns in your aura must make Wisdom save or be Frightened for 1 minute.
