**Hit Point Die:** d6
**Saving Throws:** Constitution, Charisma

### Key Features

**Spellcasting (Level 1):** Charisma-based. Use an Arcane Focus. Change one prepared spell per level-up.

**Innate Sorcery (Level 1):** Bonus Action — activate for 1 minute: spell save DC +1, Advantage on Sorcerer spell attack rolls. 2 uses; recharge on Long Rest.

**Font of Magic (Level 2):** Gain Sorcery Points (= Sorcerer level). Convert spell slots to Sorcery Points or vice versa.

**Creating Spell Slots:** 1 SP = level 1 (min Sorc 2), 3 SP = level 2 (min 3), 5 SP = level 3 (min 5), 6 SP = level 4 (min 7), 7 SP = level 5 (min 9). Max level 5.

**Metamagic (Level 2):** Gain 2 options; +2 at level 10, +2 at level 17. Options:
- **Careful Spell (1 SP):** Up to Cha modifier creatures auto-succeed on saves.
- **Distant Spell (1 SP):** Double range or change Touch to 30 ft.
- **Empowered Spell (1 SP):** Reroll up to Cha modifier damage dice.
- **Extended Spell (1 SP):** Double duration (max 24 hours); Advantage on Concentration saves.
- **Heightened Spell (2 SP):** One target has Disadvantage on saves.
- **Quickened Spell (2 SP):** Change casting time from action to Bonus Action (no level 1+ spell on same turn).
- **Seeking Spell (1 SP):** Reroll a missed attack roll spell.
- **Subtle Spell (1 SP):** Cast without Verbal, Somatic, or unconsumed Material components.
- **Transmuted Spell (1 SP):** Change damage type among Acid, Cold, Fire, Lightning, Poison, Thunder.
- **Twinned Spell (1 SP):** Increase spell's effective level by 1 to target an additional creature.

**Sorcerous Restoration (Level 5):** On Short Rest, regain up to half Sorcerer level (round down) Sorcery Points. Once per Long Rest.

**Sorcery Incarnate (Level 7):** Spend 2 SP to activate Innate Sorcery when out of uses. While active, use up to two Metamagic options per spell.

**Epic Boon (Level 19):** Gain an Epic Boon feat (Boon of Dimensional Travel recommended).

**Arcane Apotheosis (Level 20):** While Innate Sorcery is active, use one Metamagic option per turn for free.

### Subclasses

#### Aberrant Sorcery
*Psionic Spells:* Arms of Hadar, Calm Emotions, Detect Thoughts, Dissonant Whispers, Mind Sliver (3); Hunger of Hadar, Sending (5); Evard's Black Tentacles, Summon Aberration (7); Rary's Telepathic Bond, Telekinesis (9).
- **Telepathic Speech (Level 3):** Bonus Action — establish telepathic link with one creature for Sorcerer level minutes.
- **Psionic Sorcery (Level 6):** Cast Psionic Spells by spending SP = spell level instead of a slot (no V/S components).
- **Psychic Defenses (Level 6):** Resistance to Psychic damage; Advantage on saves vs. Charmed/Frightened.
- **Revelation in Flesh (Level 14):** Bonus Action + SP — alter your body for 10 minutes (1 SP each): Aquatic Adaptation, Glistening Flight, See the Invisible, or Wormlike Movement.
- **Warping Implosion (Level 18):** Magic Action — teleport 120 ft.; creatures in your previous space make Strength save or take 3d10 Force damage and get pulled to that space.

#### Clockwork Sorcery
*Clockwork Spells:* Aid, Alarm, Lesser Restoration, Protection from Evil and Good (3); Dispel Magic, Protection from Energy (5); Freedom of Movement, Summon Construct (7); Greater Restoration, Wall of Force (9).
- **Restore Balance (Level 3):** Reaction — cancel Advantage or Disadvantage on an upcoming D20 roll within 60 ft. Uses = Cha modifier; recharge on Long Rest.
- **Bastion of Law (Level 6):** Magic Action + 1–5 SP — create a ward on a creature (d8s = SP spent) that can absorb incoming damage.
- **Trance of Order (Level 14):** Bonus Action for 1 minute — attack rolls against you can't benefit from Advantage; treat d20 rolls ≤9 as 10.
- **Clockwork Cavalcade (Level 18):** Magic Action — spectral Construct spirits appear in 30-ft. Cube: heal up to 100 HP total, repair all damaged objects, end all spells of level 6 or lower.

#### Draconic Sorcery
*Draconic Spells:* Alter Self, Chromatic Orb, Command, Dragon's Breath (3); Fear, Fly (5); Arcane Eye, Charm Monster (7); Legend Lore, Summon Dragon (9).
- **Draconic Resilience (Level 3):** HP max +3, +1 per level. Unarmored AC = 10 + Dex + Cha.
- **Elemental Affinity (Level 6):** Choose one damage type (Acid, Cold, Fire, Lightning, Poison) — gain Resistance and add Cha modifier to one damage roll of matching spells.
- **Dragon Wings (Level 14):** Bonus Action — gain 60-ft. Fly Speed for 1 hour. Once per Long Rest or 3 SP.
- **Dragon Companion (Level 18):** Cast Summon Dragon without material components or a slot (once per Long Rest); can make it non-Concentration (lasts 1 minute).

#### Wild Magic Sorcery
- **Wild Magic Surge (Level 3):** After casting a Sorcerer spell with a slot, can roll d20; on 20, roll on the Wild Magic Surge table.
- **Tides of Chaos (Level 3):** Gain Advantage on one D20 Test; must cast a spell or Long Rest to reuse (auto-triggers a Surge if you cast before resting).
- **Bend Luck (Level 6):** Reaction + 1 SP — roll 1d4 and add or subtract it from another creature's D20 Test.
- **Controlled Chaos (Level 14):** Roll twice on the Wild Magic Surge table and choose either result.
- **Tamed Surge (Level 18):** After casting with a slot, choose any Wild Magic Surge effect instead of rolling (once per Long Rest).
